    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Jacksonville, NC

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s.     PEX for Water Line Replacement in Jacksonville

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, often a foot and a half to three feet underground.     Copper to PEX Conversions in Jacksonville

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Well owners around Jacksonville benefit from periodic water testing and, when needed, neutralizers or filters that protect pipes and appliances. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Jacksonville, NC

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe.     Leak Repair with PEX in Jacksonville, NC

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two.     Uponor PEX System Installation in Jacksonville

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Jacksonville, NC

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.
