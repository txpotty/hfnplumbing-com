    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Jacksonville

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Jacksonville

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Washbrook and Bardon models come in several flush ratings, and the valve has to match the bowl. Matching is simple with the spec sheet. We order both together.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Jacksonville, NC

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. American Standard, Kohler, Zurn and Sloan all make reliable urinals, and we steer customers toward models whose flush valves and cartridges are stocked at local supply houses so a repair never waits on shipping. We install all of them. Spec sheets come first.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Jacksonville, NC

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Jacksonville

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Jacksonville, NC

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go. A 2 inch drain, a vent tie-in and a supply sized to the valve go in, with blocking or a compact carrier. Many use 0.125 gallons per flush. That barely touches the water bill.

    Office and Restaurant Urinal Installation in Jacksonville

    Restaurant restrooms see a lot of traffic in short bursts. A single urinal at a busy bar can flush a few hundred times on a Friday night, so we install fixtures and valves that recover quickly and stand up to rough handling without loosening on the wall. Vandal-resistant valves are available. Night installs keep you open.
