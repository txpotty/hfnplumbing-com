    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Jacksonville, NC

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. The crew saturates a felt tube with two-part epoxy on a roller table at the truck, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Solutions in Jacksonville

    Perma-Liner makes inversion drums and epoxy systems for lining 2- to 6-inch drain lines, which covers most of what we see in houses. The equipment fits in a pickup bed. Compact gear matters on tight lots. Resin gets mixed on site and timed to the length of the run, so it does not start curing before the liner is in place.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Repair in Jacksonville, NC

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom. Lining it from a cleanout avoids saw-cutting the floor inside an occupied tenant space. Work gets scheduled around tenant hours, nights included. A typical commercial lining job runs one to three days, depending on footage and how many branch connections need to be reinstated with a cutter.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Repairs in Jacksonville

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Jacksonville

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.

    Pipe Bursting in Jacksonville, NC

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. Before pulling, the crew heat-fuses sections of HDPE into one continuous length on the ground. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. A 60-foot pull often takes under an hour.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Jacksonville

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line to remove roots and grease, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Jacksonville

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both systems are made to meet the ASTM standards written for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. So the line gets jetted or descaled to bare wall before any resin goes in.
