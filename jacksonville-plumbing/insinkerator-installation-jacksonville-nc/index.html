    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Jacksonville

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We drive the plug out and fish it out of the chamber. The hose clamps onto the inlet and runs through an air gap or a high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from siphoning back into the tub. We test both appliances at once.

    Badger Disposal Install in Jacksonville

    Most replacement disposals in area kitchens are a Badger. A 1/3 to 3/4 HP Badger handles normal scraps and fits almost any sink base cabinet. The unit hangs on its quick-lock mount, the discharge tube goes on, a cord kit gets added if needed and we finish with a cold-water test grind. About an hour, usually.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Jacksonville

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    Hard Water and Disposals in Jacksonville

    Municipal water in North Carolina is generally soft, but many private wells produce acidic water that can corrode copper pipe over time. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. Drainage slows too, since crust narrows the tailpiece and trap, and we check those parts before tying a new unit into them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Jacksonville, NC

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot. What they notice later is fewer clogs downstream, because the multi-stage grind breaks fibrous scraps into much smaller pieces before they reach the trap and the branch line. We check for vibration with the unit running.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Jacksonville

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. We wire each one to match.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in Jacksonville, NC

    Old disposals are heavy and awkward to get rid of. They leave on our truck. The old cord, any replaced mount ring and the rusty slip nuts go too, so you are not stuck with a bag of parts on trash day. One visit handles most of these.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Jacksonville, NC

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.
