    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Jacksonville

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Jacksonville, NC

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Jacksonville, NC

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. A cracked riser lets surface water and grit into the interceptor.     Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Jacksonville

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Food solids packed against the baffle get scraped out by hand. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream.     Flow Control Device Repair in Jacksonville

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Take it out and a three-compartment sink dump blows grease right past the baffles. Inspectors look for it. The air intake gets cleared too, because the fitting needs air to work.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Jacksonville

We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.
