    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Jacksonville, NC

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst.     Traditional Water Line Excavation in Jacksonville

    Sometimes the open trench wins. A collapsed line, a route with sharp bends, or solid rock can rule out trenchless. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, lay the new pipe on a sand bed, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Jacksonville, NC

    Pipe pulling works like this. Two pits get dug, one at each end. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, splitting the old pipe if it is brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.

    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in Jacksonville, NC

    A lot of older houses were fed with a 1/2-inch or 3/4-inch line that was fine for one bathroom. Add a second bath and a dishwasher and it falls short. When we replace the line we size it to your fixture count, often stepping up to 1-inch pipe, and bring the shutoff, depth and backflow pieces up to what code asks for today. Better pressure upstairs.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Jacksonville

    We buy PEX in long coils. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Jacksonville

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Burst Main Line Response in Jacksonville, NC

    Burst lines do damage fast. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the line is too far gone to bother patching. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.
