    Why Choose HFN Plumbing for Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Jacksonville, NC?

    Municipal water in North Carolina is generally soft, but many private wells produce acidic water that can corrode copper pipe over time. Hard water scales up heat exchangers, and most early tankless failures we see trace back to scale and skipped flushing. Every install gets isolation valves. A scale filter helps too.

    • Long Lifespan, With yearly flushing, a tankless often lasts close to 20 years, about twice a typical tank.
    • Right-Sized Units, We calculate gallons per minute and temperature rise for your fixtures instead of estimating from square footage.
    • Efficiency, No standby loss means lower fuel use keeping water hot that nobody is using.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Jacksonville, NC

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Jacksonville, NC

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Built-in pumps on Navien A and some Rinnai models push water through a crossover valve or a dedicated return. We set a schedule. Mornings and evenings, usually.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Jacksonville, NC

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. The old flue gets capped.

    Navien Venting and Install in Jacksonville

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Warranty depends on it.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Jacksonville, NC

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. We set up both.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Jacksonville, NC

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Jacksonville

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Jacksonville

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. Then we look at whether the leak is a fitting or the heat exchanger itself, because one is a $20 fix and the other means a new unit.
