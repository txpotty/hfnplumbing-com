    Disposal Installation in Jacksonville, NC: How We Do It

    Disposal Upgrades in Jacksonville, NC

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request. We disconnect and remove the old disposal, check the trap and drain arm for corrosion, and install the new unit with a new mount, so the upgrade does not inherit the old unit's worn parts. Corroded drain arms get swapped. Galvanized arms rust from the inside.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Jacksonville

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Jacksonville, NC

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Jacksonville, NC

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Jacksonville, NC

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. We haul the old unit away.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Jacksonville

    With a typical 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Jacksonville homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Tight cabinets need planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    Commercial Disposal Setup in Jacksonville

    Restaurants and commercial kitchens in Jacksonville need disposals built to run all day. They usually need their own circuit, a bigger drain and a panel that runs water on its own, and some sewer districts restrict them, so we confirm the rules first. Every hour down hurts.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Jacksonville

    These disposals run only with the stopper lid seated, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.
