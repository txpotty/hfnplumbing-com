    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger.     Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Jacksonville, NC

    Needing two flushes on a regular basis usually comes down to scale in the rim jets, a clogged siphon jet, a partial blockage in the trapway, or a flush valve that does not open all the way.     Flush Mechanism Repair in Jacksonville, NC

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Repair in Jacksonville

    Most simple toilet repairs, a new flapper or fill valve, cost about $125 to $275 installed. Resetting a toilet on a new wax ring usually runs $175 to $350, and a flange repair can bring the total to $300 to $500. The price is set before we start.

    Flush only paper, and keep wipes, floss, paper towels and cotton swabs in the trash where they belong. Deal with a running toilet as soon as you notice it, because the leak wears the flush valve seat rougher over time and turns a cheap flapper job into a full flush valve replacement. Check the supply line once a year.

    A loose chain or a float set too high is a fair DIY fix, and so is swapping a flapper if you buy the right size for your flush valve. Bring the old flapper with you to match it. If the toilet still runs after that, the seat or the fill valve is usually the problem, and that is a good time to call HFN Plumbing.

    Constant running, a weak flush, water at the base and a bowl that rocks are the big four. Gurgling or whistling from the tank points to a valve problem, and a hairline crack in the porcelain should be looked at before it spreads. Cracks are the one sign you should not sit on.

    Watch for phantom refills at night, a hiss from the tank, a toilet that shifts when you sit, and stains or soft flooring around the base. Repeated clogs belong on that list too. Catching these early keeps a flapper job from turning into a subfloor repair.

    Every repair HFN Plumbing does in Jacksonville, NC carries a workmanship guarantee, and the parts carry their own manufacturer coverage on top of that. You will see the warranty terms in writing on the invoice. Keep it with your house papers.
