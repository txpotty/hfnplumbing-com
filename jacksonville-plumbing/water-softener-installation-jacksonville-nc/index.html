    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Jacksonville

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Jacksonville

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Jacksonville

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Jacksonville, NC

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Jacksonville, NC

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Filters add cost. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Jacksonville, NC

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Softener Replacement in Jacksonville, NC

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Salt use drops. Performance comes back right away.

    Softener Loop Installation in Jacksonville

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Jacksonville

    Most installs cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop usually adds $500 to $1,500, depending on how easy it is to reach the main line.

    Standard residential softener installs often need no separate permit. Major main line or drain changes can need one, and we let you know. We handle the paperwork.

    Softeners with Fleck and Clack control valves are what we install most. We recommend systems based on performance and how easy they are to service later.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners alter the minerals instead, and softeners are stronger for very hard water.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. A yearly checkup with a plumber catches worn seals, salt bridges in the brine tank and clogged injectors before they cause hard water. Keep the salt level up.

    We install Culligan, Pelican, Fleck, Kenmore, Whirlpool and GE, among others. You can pick with our help based on water and price.

    Salt use depends on hardness, water use and how efficient the softener is. A family of four with fairly hard water might use 40 to 80 pounds of salt per month, and a metered high-efficiency model uses less. Metered valves save most.

    Most homeowners are better off hiring a pro. Bad installs cause leaks, poor softening or slow damage to the plumbing that nobody notices until a fitting lets go behind the water heater. A plumber handles sizing and code.
