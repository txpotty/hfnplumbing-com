    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Jacksonville

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Jacksonville, NC

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Jacksonville, NC

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking.     Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Jacksonville, NC
