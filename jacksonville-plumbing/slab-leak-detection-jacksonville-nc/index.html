    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Jacksonville, NC

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Jacksonville

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Jacksonville, NC home.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Jacksonville, NC

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. Most repipes wrap up in two to four days.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Jacksonville, NC

    Under-slab drains crack as well. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, which is why we push a camera through to look for cracks, bellies and pulled joints. You see the footage yourself before any repair talk starts in your Jacksonville home.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Jacksonville, NC

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Jacksonville, NC home.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Jacksonville, NC

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. It shrinks the search area before we listen.
