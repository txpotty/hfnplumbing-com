    A valve-only swap costs far less than a full rebuild. It only makes sense when the pan and walls are sound, though, because a new valve in a shower with a failing pan just moves the leak to a different spot. New pans usually mean new tile. The condition decides the fix.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Jacksonville, NC

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. The drain moves to the pan's center, the valve goes up to shower height and the slope keeps water from escaping the entry. Grab bar blocking is easy now. Accessibility standards are followed on request.

    Showerhead and Trim Upgrade in Jacksonville, NC

    If the valve is sound, new trim and a new head can refresh the shower without opening the wall. A trim kit matched to your valve, a new head and an optional slide bar update the look without demo. Rubber nozzles wipe clean. It costs the least.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Jacksonville

    Old valves waste water and swing temperature. Corroded valves get swapped for pressure-balance or thermostatic models, often from an access panel behind the wall. Larger trim plates cover old openings. It can be part of a full replacement or a stand-alone upgrade.

    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Jacksonville

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. The tub comes out, the drain is adjusted and the valve goes in at the right height with grab bar blocking and a handheld. Thresholds stay low. It is one of the safest changes you can make.
