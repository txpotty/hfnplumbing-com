    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Jacksonville, NC

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life.     Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Jacksonville

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age.     CSST Repair and Bonding in Jacksonville, NC

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic.     Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Jacksonville, NC

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Jacksonville, NC
