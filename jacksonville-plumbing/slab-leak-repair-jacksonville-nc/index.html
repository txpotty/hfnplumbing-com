    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Jacksonville

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it.     Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Jacksonville

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Jacksonville home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Jacksonville

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. It finds rebar and cables. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Jacksonville.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Jacksonville, NC

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Jacksonville, NC

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper tends to pinhole. Copper wears through at rub points or from water chemistry, and PEX usually leaks at a poorly crimped fitting or where a rodent got to it. Trucks in Jacksonville, NC stock fittings for each type.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Jacksonville

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Jacksonville, NC

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.
