    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Jacksonville

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Older homes around Jacksonville may have galvanized steel supply lines or cast iron drains that are reaching the end of their life. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. We price each one for you.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Jacksonville, NC

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. A large share of homes across the state rely on private wells, and iron and manganese often leave orange or dark stains on fixtures. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.
