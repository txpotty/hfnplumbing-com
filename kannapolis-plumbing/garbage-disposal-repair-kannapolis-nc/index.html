    Leaking Disposal Fix in Kannapolis, NC

    A drip at the top usually means the sink flange putty has dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. A leak on the side is usually the discharge gasket or a loose hose clamp.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Kannapolis

    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Kannapolis

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Kannapolis

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Most smells start at the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Kannapolis

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Kannapolis

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. After checking the chamber, snugging the mount and pulling debris, we run it empty to hear if the noise is coming from the motor. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit.
