    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Kannapolis, NC

    Retail and office buildings have long horizontal drain runs that collect buildup. Low-slope lines clog more. We jet those runs on a schedule that matches the building's use, and we can work after hours so tenants are not dealing with equipment in hallways or restrooms during the day.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Kannapolis

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Kannapolis

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Main Line Jetting in Kannapolis, NC

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Kannapolis, NC

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Kannapolis

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Plan on two to three hours.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Kannapolis

    Backups at night or on weekends are handled with the same gear as any weekday call. Our on-call trucks carry jetters. We can clear and jet a line in one visit instead of returning Monday.
