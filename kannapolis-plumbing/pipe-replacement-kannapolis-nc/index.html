    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Choices in Kannapolis, NC

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Kannapolis

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Disconnecting garden hoses and insulating exposed lines are easy steps that help homes around Kannapolis get through a cold night. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Kannapolis, NC

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Kannapolis, NC

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, and we check the water heater connections and shutoffs since they often corrode too. Look at the joints.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Kannapolis

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Well owners around Kannapolis benefit from periodic water testing and, when needed, neutralizers or filters that protect pipes and appliances. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Kannapolis

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.
