    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Greensboro

    Most installs cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. A new loop typically adds $500 to $1,500 based on access to the main.

    Standard residential softener installs often need no separate permit. If the job involves major changes to the main line or the drain system, a permit may be required, and we will tell you before we start. We handle the paperwork.

    Manufacturer coverage on the unit is often 5 to 10 years on major parts. We cover our labor, usually for one year, and install to spec to keep the unit warranty valid.

    We install Culligan, Pelican, Fleck, Kenmore, Whirlpool and GE, among others. We help you choose the right brand and model for your water and budget.

    Salt-based softeners add a small amount of sodium. Plenty of homeowners add an RO system at the kitchen sink for drinking and cooking.

    A softener targets calcium and magnesium to stop scale. A filter removes things like chlorine and sediment to improve taste and safety, and plenty of homes use both together.
