    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Greensboro

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Greensboro

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Badger Series Installation in Greensboro, NC

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. All of them use the same quick-lock mount. Because of that, a Badger-for-Badger swap can take under an hour, and the flange often stays if it is still clean. Badgers ship without a cord, so we bring the cord kit.
