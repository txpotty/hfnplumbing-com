    CSST Repair and Bonding in Greensboro, NC

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. It takes about an hour. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Hurricanes and tropical storms can bring heavy rain and flooding to North Carolina, especially in coastal and low-lying areas.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Greensboro

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Greensboro, NC

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Greensboro

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Coastal areas have sandy soil and high water tables, which affect how sewer lines and septic systems function. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    CSST Repair in Greensboro

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. Bonding gets checked on every system. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Greensboro, NC

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it.
