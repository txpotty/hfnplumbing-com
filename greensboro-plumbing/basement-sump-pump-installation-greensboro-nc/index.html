    Sump Pit Installation in Greensboro, NC

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. A small 15 inch pit makes the pump cycle every minute in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Emergency Sump Pump Installation in Greensboro, NC

    A dead pump with water rising in the pit is a same-day call. The plumber shows up with a replacement pump already loaded, pulls the old unit, checks whether the float or the check valve caused the failure, and gets water moving again in Greensboro, NC. Bail with a bucket until we arrive.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Greensboro

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Greensboro homes. Expect more noise.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Greensboro

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house. We add a spring check valve so the water in the pipe cannot fall back, which cuts motor run time and the loud thunk you hear after every cycle. Hard freezes are uncommon along the coast but happen most winters in the mountains, where pipes in crawlspaces and outside walls require protection. We fit a freeze-resistant outlet.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Greensboro

    Zoeller's M53 Mighty-Mate is the cast iron pump we install more than any other. It runs at 3/10 horsepower on a vertical float switch, and it moves about 2,580 gallons an hour at 5 feet of lift, enough for a lot of Greensboro basements. Parts are easy to get.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Greensboro, NC

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. You hear far less of them than a pedestal, and in Greensboro, NC we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Greensboro, NC

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Hurricanes and tropical storms can bring heavy rain and flooding to North Carolina, especially in coastal and low-lying areas. A second pump goes into the same pit on its own float switch, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. Your primary pump still handles the everyday load.
