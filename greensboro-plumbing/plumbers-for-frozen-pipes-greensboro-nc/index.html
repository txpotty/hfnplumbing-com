    Residential Frozen Pipe Thawing in Greensboro

    A faucet that trickles or goes dry on a cold morning points to ice somewhere in its supply line. We trace the line back with a thermal camera to find the cold spot. Once we know where it is, we thaw it with controlled heat while the faucet stays open, so the melting water has a way out and pressure cannot build behind the ice.

    Freeze Protection for Commercial Buildings in Greensboro, NC

    Vacant suites and warehouses freeze when thermostats are set too low. Property managers get a freeze plan. That plan lists the shutoffs, the at-risk runs, the heat cable and the minimum thermostat setting for each space, so building staff know exactly what to do when a cold front is in the forecast.

    Hose Bib and Outdoor Faucet Repair in Greensboro, NC

    Outdoor faucets freeze easily, especially with a hose left connected. A split often hides inside the wall. You will not see it until the next time you turn on the faucet in spring and water pours into the basement or crawlspace, so we replace split hose bibs with frost-free models that shut off inside the heated wall.

    Post-Thaw Pipe Inspection and Repair in Greensboro, NC

    A pipe that froze and thawed without leaking can still have a hidden crack. We pressure test it to find out. With the line isolated and a gauge on it, even a hairline split shows up as a pressure drop within minutes, and finding it now beats finding it later as a stain on the ceiling.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Greensboro, NC

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a spigot with a hose still attached. The hose traps water in the valve. Irrigation backflow devices sit fully exposed, so we drain or cover them before winter and repair split ones.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Greensboro, NC

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. For truly exposed runs, we add heat cable. Self-regulating cable on a GFCI outlet, covered with insulation, keeps the pipe above freezing.

    Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Greensboro, NC

    Water spraying from a wall or ceiling is a burst pipe. Close the main shutoff right away. Our plumber cuts out the damaged section, replaces it with new copper or PEX, and pressure tests the whole branch before turning water back on, since one freeze often splits a pipe in more than one place.
