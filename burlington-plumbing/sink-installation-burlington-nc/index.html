    Undermount Sink Installation in Burlington

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Burlington, NC

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. Undermounts cost more for clips and cure time, and vessels add a special drain and a taller faucet. Moving the drain adds the most. We price each piece on the quote.
