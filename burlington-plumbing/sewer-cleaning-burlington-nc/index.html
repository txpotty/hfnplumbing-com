    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Burlington

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Burlington, NC

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Much of the housing in North Carolina was built after the 1970s, and many of those homes use CPVC, copper or PEX supply lines. The bottom of the pipe wears first.
