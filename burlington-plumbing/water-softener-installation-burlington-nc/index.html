    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Burlington

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Burlington, NC

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Burlington

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Burlington, NC

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Burlington, NC

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Burlington, NC

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HFN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.
