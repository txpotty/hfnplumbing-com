    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Burlington, NC

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. HFN Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Burlington

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Burlington, NC

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. HFN Plumbing repairs these units in Burlington, NC. Media lasts a few years.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Burlington

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Burlington, NC

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Commercial Water Softener Repair in Burlington, NC

    Laundromats and restaurants in Burlington, NC run through soft water fast, and a dishwasher or boiler starts scaling the moment the softener stops working. Every day of downtime costs money. HFN Plumbing repairs commercial-grade softeners, from twin-tank setups to big valve heads. We schedule around business hours.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Burlington, NC

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Keep the salt about half full.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Burlington

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.
