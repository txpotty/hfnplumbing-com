    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Burlington

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. Good prep makes a good liner.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Burlington

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Burlington

    Older Burlington homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Burlington, NC

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. HFN Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Burlington

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Burlington yard.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Burlington

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Coastal areas have sandy soil and high water tables, which affect how sewer lines and septic systems function. We fix minor cracks with a spot liner. For longer damage we line the whole run, and for a collapse we burst in new pipe, without trenching the yard or cutting the drive.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Burlington, NC

    Per foot of pipe, trenchless can cost more than open trenching. That's not the whole bill. Once you add restoring driveways, patios, landscaping and irrigation, trenchless is often the better value, and it is faster and far less disruptive for the household. We compare both with real numbers for your home.

    Sectional Point Repair in Burlington

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Burlington sewer lines.
