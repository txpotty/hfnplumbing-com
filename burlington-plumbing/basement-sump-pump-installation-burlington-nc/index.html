    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Burlington

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid. A submersible with a gasketed cover keeps the noise down, and we route the discharge behind framing or through a closet so the pipe does not run across a wall you just painted in your Burlington home. We patch the slab flush.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Burlington

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Burlington homes. Expect more noise.

    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Burlington, NC

    High water alarms cost little and buy you time. The basic version sounds off when water climbs above the float, while a Wi-Fi monitor sends a text if the level rises or the power drops, which homeowners who travel tend to like. We test it in front of you.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Burlington, NC

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. For those, we step up to a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower unit in a bigger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. Timing the refill comes first.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Burlington

    No pit yet? First we locate the low point where water gathers, saw cut the slab, dig roughly 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a liner surrounded by washed gravel so water can enter while fine soil stays out. New concrete goes back flush and level.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Burlington

    Zoeller's M53 Mighty-Mate is the cast iron pump we install more than any other. It is a 3/10 horsepower unit with a vertical float switch, and it moves about 2,580 gallons an hour at 5 feet of lift, enough for a lot of Burlington basements. Replacement switches are on every supply shelf.

    Emergency Sump Pump Installation in Burlington, NC

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Burlington, NC

The main pump keeps doing the daily work.
