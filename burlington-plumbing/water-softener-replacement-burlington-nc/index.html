    Picking the Right Softener for Burlington, NC Homes

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Burlington

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Burlington, NC

    Swapping a salt-based softener in North Carolina homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Burlington, NC

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Burlington

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Burlington, NC

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. We set it before we leave.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Burlington

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Burlington, NC

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. A metered valve with an efficient brine setting can cut salt use by a third or more compared with an old timer unit, and that adds up over fifteen years. We compare brands on that basis.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Burlington

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Softener Replacement Near You in Burlington, NC

    Most softener jobs around Burlington are finished in an afternoon. Our trucks stock the common grain sizes, which means many replacements happen the day you call. Bigger systems take a day to order.

    Local water varies. Hardness can be very different between a city supply and a private well a few miles out, and iron or chlorine changes what else the system needs. We plan for what you actually have.
