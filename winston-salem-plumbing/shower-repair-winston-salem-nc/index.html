    Fixing Showers in Winston-Salem, NC

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Winston-Salem

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Winston-Salem, NC

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Winston-Salem, NC

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Winston-Salem, NC

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. A sudden leak behind the wall or a valve that will not close can soak framing fast, so the main valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house, is your first stop. Then call us. Our plumber stops the water, fixes it or makes it safe and checks for wet areas.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Winston-Salem, NC

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Winston-Salem, NC

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Winston-Salem, NC

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Next, call us. HFN Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Winston-Salem, NC

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.
