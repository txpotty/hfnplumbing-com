    Water Heater Maintenance in Winston-Salem

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Municipal water in North Carolina is generally soft, but many private wells produce acidic water that can eat away at copper pipe over time. The tank gets flushed, the anode rod gets checked, the relief valve gets tested and the connections get inspected. Flushing takes about an hour. Less sediment means quicker heating and a longer tank life.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspection in Winston-Salem, NC

    Room by room, fixture by fixture. We check under every sink for drips, test toilet fill valves and flappers, run every faucet and shower, look at the washer hoses and read the house water pressure at a hose bib. Washers and flappers get replaced that day. Larger repairs get a written price.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Winston-Salem

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. Sediment drain, anode check, burner or element inspection and a relief valve test cover both tank and tankless. Cold showers are avoidable. We spot the signs early.

    Softener Upkeep in Winston-Salem, NC

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. We check the bypass and drain line. Then we test the treated water.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Winston-Salem

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. We pump descaler through the service valves. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Winston-Salem, NC

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Mineral buildup on the bottom wastes fuel and can lead to early tank failure.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Winston-Salem

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. Camera checks and cleaning on problem lines keep grease and roots from causing a holiday backup. Jetting helps on grease. Root-prone lines go on a schedule.
