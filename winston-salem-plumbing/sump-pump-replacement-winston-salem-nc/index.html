    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Winston-Salem, NC Home

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Winston-Salem, NC

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. The fix is to cut the slab, drop in a bigger poly basin with a bolted lid, and patch the concrete. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts mean the motor lasts longer.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Winston-Salem

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Winston-Salem, NC

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. If you have a Zoeller now, we can usually match the discharge outlet and switch style so the swap goes quickly. Need more lift? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Winston-Salem

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. Both pumps and the alternator get swapped together. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Winston-Salem, NC

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. Narrow pits, under about 12 inches across, are where they make the most sense.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Winston-Salem, NC

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. The tech brings a replacement pump and a new check valve and can have water moving out of the pit within an hour of arrival in most cases. Then we find out why it failed.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Winston-Salem

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. Then a new check valve goes on. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Winston-Salem

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Figure on a couple of hours.

    Local Sump Pump Replacement in Winston-Salem, NC

    Local drainage matters. We check the yard grade and the discharge outlet, since water that drains back toward the foundation just gets pumped again and again. Sometimes the fix is ten feet of pipe.
