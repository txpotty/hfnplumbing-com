    Running Toilet Fixes in Winston-Salem

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Winston-Salem, NC

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We set the water level to the mark on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A loose or cracked handle is a five-minute swap.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Winston-Salem, NC

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some.     Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Winston-Salem, NC

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Winston-Salem, NC
