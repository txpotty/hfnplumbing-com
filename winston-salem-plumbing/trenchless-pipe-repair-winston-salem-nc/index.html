    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Winston-Salem

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Winston-Salem

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Winston-Salem yard.
