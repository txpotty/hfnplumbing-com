    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Mooresville

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster. Pumping and hauling are both on us. Our vacuum trucks pull the full contents of the trap into a sealed tank, and the load gets logged with gallons and destination. No barrels left behind.

    Grease Line Jetting in Mooresville

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. The buildup forms rings that are thickest along the bottom. A snake cuts a narrow path through and the line plugs again within weeks, while a jetter with a rotating nozzle peels the grease off the whole pipe wall. A camera confirms it afterward.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Mooresville, NC

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. A jetter takes it off the pipe wall. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Mooresville, NC

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. Every compartment gets opened and pumped to the bottom. While the tank is empty, we look at the inlet and outlet tees, the baffle wall, the risers and the lid seals, because a cracked riser lets groundwater and dirt in. Lids go back on tight.

    Compliance Documentation for FOG Programs in Mooresville, NC

    Inspectors usually ask for at least a year of pumping records. Missing records can count as a violation even if the trap is clean. Our service sheets carry the date, the gallons removed, the measured depths and the hauler's name, and we can email copies to a manager or a corporate office on every visit. Keep the originals on site.
