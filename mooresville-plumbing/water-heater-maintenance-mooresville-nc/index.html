    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Mooresville, NC

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense.     Water Heater Leak Checks in Mooresville, NC

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust trails mean trouble. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Mooresville

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Mooresville

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Mooresville, NC

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Mooresville

    Location changes the risks. Attic units need a working drain pan and drain line so a leak does not come through the ceiling, and garage gas units often need to be raised off the floor or protected from vehicles under local code. We check the pan and drain. Access platforms and venting get looked at too.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Mooresville

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Anode Rod Checks in Mooresville

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.
