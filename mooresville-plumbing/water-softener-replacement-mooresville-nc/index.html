    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Mooresville

    A single-tank softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual-tank or premium systems run $3,000 to $5,000. A new loop or drain line adds to that, sometimes by several hundred dollars depending on where the pipes run.

    Salt-based softeners swap calcium and magnesium for sodium through ion exchange, so the water actually tests soft. Salt-free conditioners leave the minerals in the water but change their structure so they are less likely to stick to pipes and heating elements. Only the salt unit softens.

    Spots and crust returning after a few years of clean fixtures usually means the softener stopped working. First look at the salt, because a level that never drops means the system is not regenerating. Age matters too.

    Fleck, Autotrol, Pelican and Watts systems are all on our list, along with Clack valves on many of the systems we build. Parts support drives our choice. A valve you can rebuild is worth more.

    Five to ten years on the valve is common, with longer coverage on the tanks. Our labor is covered separately.

    It looks simple from the box. You are cutting into the main, setting an air-gapped drain and programming the valve, and a slip on any of those means leaks or hard water. A pro install avoids that.

    Two warranties apply. Tanks and valves carry a manufacturer warranty, usually 5 to 10 years or more. HFN Plumbing adds a labor warranty on the installation.
