    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Mooresville

    Soggy grass near the front walk or a sinking spot over the line can mean the service pipe is leaking. Old galvanized and copper lines fail most.     Gas Line Leak Locating in Mooresville

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution.     Preventive Leak Inspections in Mooresville, NC

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors benefit most from a yearly check, because those parts often fail together. Short visit, big savings.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Mooresville
