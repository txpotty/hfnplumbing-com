    Pipe Bursting Services in Mooresville, NC

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Mooresville, NC soil.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Mooresville

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Mooresville yard.
