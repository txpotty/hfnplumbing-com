    Sewer Camera Inspection in Mooresville

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. It shows us everything. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, and the transmitter lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above it. It saves guessing where to dig. You get a copy of the footage.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Mooresville

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Many times they team up. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then camera the line to see which one started it.

    Tree Root Invasion Removal in Mooresville

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture in the soil. They only need a tiny opening. Once inside, they spread into a net that traps everything moving through. We cut them out with a root saw on a sectional machine, then camera the line to see how many joints are letting roots in. Lining or replacement seals the joints for good.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Mooresville

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That is the difference. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale while the nozzle drives itself forward. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Results often last years.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Mooresville

    Some older homes have no cleanout at all. That makes every clog harder.     Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in Mooresville, NC

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Layer after layer builds up. HFN Plumbing jets it out with a grease nozzle in Mooresville, NC. The camera confirms it is gone.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Mooresville, NC

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. The pipe gets cleaned all the way around.
