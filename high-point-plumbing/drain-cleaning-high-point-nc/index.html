    Jetting Versus Cabling in High Point, NC

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. Jetting strips grease and scale off the walls and carries it out of the line, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Kitchen Drain Cleaning in High Point

    Grease is the big one in kitchens. Hot grease cools in the line and hardens on the pipe wall. Food scraps and soap stick to that layer and the opening keeps shrinking until the sink holds water, and we clear it with a drum machine that is safe for PVC and old cast iron alike.

    Main Line Drain Cleaning in High Point

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? The main line is the likely spot. Every fixture shares that one pipe, so the lowest drain in the house is where water comes up, and the usual causes are roots, a crushed section of old pipe or a heap of wipes caught on a bad joint, which we find with a camera and clear with a sectional cable or a jetter.
