    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in High Point, NC

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Much of the housing in North Carolina was built after the 1970s, and many of those homes use CPVC, copper or PEX supply lines. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Root Masses in High Point Sewer Laterals

    Jetting a Sewer Line in High Point, NC

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in High Point

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Apartment and Strip Center Sewers in High Point, NC
