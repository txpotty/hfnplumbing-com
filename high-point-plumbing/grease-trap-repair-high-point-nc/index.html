    Why Choose HFN Plumbing for Grease Trap Repair in High Point, NC?

    FOG, short for fats, oils and grease, is the reason the trap exists. Once it gets past the trap, it hardens in the sewer. Most cities run a pretreatment program that inspects grease traps on a schedule and fines kitchens whose traps are damaged, missing parts or overdue for pumping. Repairs keep you out of that file.

    • Preventive Advice, We measure the trap and recommend how often to pump that fits your kitchen.
    • Quality Parts, Manufacturer parts or commercial-grade materials rated for grease and heat.

    Citywide Coverage

    Indoor traps under three-compartment sinks and outdoor interceptors under the parking lot are both on our list. Both kinds come through our schedule every week. For buried tanks we bring a vacuum truck partner, and for indoor units the plumber can usually pump the trap by hand into sealed drums and carry it out the back door.     Experienced Team

    Diagnosing a grease trap is mostly about knowing what normal looks like. A healthy trap has a grease cap on top and clear water in the middle. Our techs measure both layers, compare them to the last pumping date, and can tell from that whether the trap is separating or just passing grease along to the sewer. The numbers tell the story.

    Safety-First Approach

    Gravity interceptors are permit-required confined spaces under OSHA rules. We never enter one without a four-gas monitor and a retrieval tripod. Hydrogen sulfide builds up inside these tanks and can knock a person out in a few breaths, so we ventilate, test the air and keep an attendant at the opening for the whole entry. We do as much as we can from the surface.

    Flow Control Device Repair in High Point

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Without it, a full sink dump pushes grease straight through. Inspectors look for it. We also clear the air intake, since a plugged intake on the flow control fitting lets the trap siphon.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in High Point

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. With old cast iron, we turn the pressure down and keep a camera on it, since a thin wall can blow out.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in High Point, NC

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. You get repair and replacement priced side by side, in writing.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in High Point

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. A jetter actually takes it out of the line.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in High Point, NC

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Older steel bodies rust out along the bottom seams, too. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Sometimes it is a $15 gasket. An opened seam is another story, and we will say up front if a patch will not hold.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in High Point
