    Picking the Right Softener for High Point, NC Water

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in High Point, NC

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. For a small house on moderately hard water, a basic metered single-tank unit often does the job for much less than a twin-tank system. We show both options side by side.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in High Point, NC

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. It depends on the valve. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in High Point, NC

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in North Carolina homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in High Point

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Softener Loop Installation in High Point, NC

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in High Point

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in High Point

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Sometimes the whole unit goes.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in High Point

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. We work around your business hours.
