    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in High Point, NC

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. You see both quotes.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in High Point, NC

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in High Point

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in High Point, NC

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in High Point, NC

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. The vent swap is part of our job. Most of the plumbing lines up already.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in High Point

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. Starved units throw error codes.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in High Point, NC

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Sudden cold snaps can catch North Carolina homeowners off guard, especially in homes with uninsulated crawlspace plumbing. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in High Point

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Corroded service valves get swapped while we are there.
