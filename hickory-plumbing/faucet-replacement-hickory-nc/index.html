    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Hickory

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. People hang buckets on the spout and yank hoses off the threads, which loosens the mounting and cracks the supply connections over time. We use brass-bodied replacements. We mount it tight to the wall or tub deck.

    Tub and Shower Trim Replacement in Hickory, NC

    Tub and shower trim can be replaced without opening the wall. A Moen Posi-Temp or Delta Monitor valve takes a matching trim kit, which swaps the handle and the spout without touching the tile. The valve has to match. An outdated valve may need replacing instead.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Hickory

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. The control box mounts on the cabinet wall above the floor, and it needs either a battery pack or an outlet the cord can reach, which some sink bases do not have. Power gets checked first. We adjust the sensor range at the end.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Hickory

    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. After a few years, the hose wears thin, the spray button sticks and the head will not dock the way it did. We swap the whole faucet. The new hose weight gets clipped where the head docks cleanly, and both supplies are new braided lines.

    Commercial Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Hickory

    Commercial kitchens are hard on faucets. We replace pre-rinse units and hand sink faucets with commercial-grade models from T&S and Chicago Faucets, which keep parts on the shelf for years. We work around your hours. Every install meets health and accessibility code.

    Emergency Faucet Replacement in Hickory, NC

    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Hickory

A hose kit can fix that. When the body or cartridge is worn too, we swap the whole faucet and put new supplies on.
