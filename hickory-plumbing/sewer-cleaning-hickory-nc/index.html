    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Hickory, NC

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. A crack stays a crack. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Hickory

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Hickory, NC

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.

    Shop and Restaurant Sewers in Hickory

    Busy buildings clog more often. Volume is the reason. We bring larger jetters for 4 and 6 inch commercial lines and can work before opening or after closing, so the cleaning does not shut down a lunch rush or a full day of business.
