    Water Softener Service in Hickory

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Hickory

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Municipal water in North Carolina is generally soft, but many private wells produce acidic water that can eat away at copper pipe over time. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Flushing takes about an hour. Less sediment means quicker heating and a longer tank life.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Hickory

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. Failed devices get rebuilt. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Late tests can mean fines.

    Sump Pump Testing in Hickory, NC

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Hurricanes and tropical storms can bring heavy rain and flooding to North Carolina, especially in coastal and low-lying areas. We fill the pit to test the float, check the discharge line and confirm the check valve holds. Battery backups get load tested.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Hickory, NC

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. Pipe material matters too. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Hickory, NC

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and grease buildup in the main and branch drains, which matters most in older homes with clay or cast iron lines that have been in the ground for decades.     Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Hickory

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. We descale tankless units through their service valves. Regular service helps keep the warranty valid.
