    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Hickory

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Hickory

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. We clear and test the drain. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Hickory, NC

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Commercial Shower Service in Hickory

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. We carry commercial cartridges and thermostatic parts, and we can stage repairs room by room so only one unit is down at a time. We can work nights. Each repaired valve gets its limit stop set and tested before we move on.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Hickory

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Hickory

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.
