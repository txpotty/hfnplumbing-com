    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Hickory

    Not every wet foundation is a plumbing leak. Gutters cause plenty.     Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Hickory

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. A cut cable can snap hard. Before we open the floor, we scan with ground-penetrating radar to map the cables, and if the leak sits too close to a tendon, rerouting the line is the safer call.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Hickory, NC

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. No floor gets cut. We dig a pit beside the foundation and hand-tunnel to the leak, which works well when the break sits under cabinets, a tiled shower or hardwood you do not want torn up. HFN Plumbing backfills and compacts the tunnel when the Hickory, NC repair is done.

    Leak Detection Tools We Use in Hickory, NC

    Infrared cameras, acoustic listening gear and tracer gas cover most leaks we chase in Hickory, NC. Gas helps when the leak is too quiet to hear. We push a hydrogen and nitrogen mix into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds pinholes that make almost no sound under thick concrete or carpet pad.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Hickory, NC

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It's usually the lowest bid. We saw a small opening, dig down to the pipe, replace the damaged section and pressure test it, then backfill in layers and patch the concrete flush with the floor around it. Your flooring contractor can lay new tile or wood afterward.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Hickory

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. Plastic pipe fails too. Most failures we find are at a fitting that was crimped short or a joint that pulled apart when the soil moved, not in the pipe wall itself. HFN Plumbing replaces the fitting with a proper crimp, expansion or solvent-weld connection so the line under your Hickory foundation is sound again.
