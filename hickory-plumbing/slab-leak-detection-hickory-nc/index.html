    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Hickory, NC

    Older homes in Hickory, NC often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both corrode with age. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. Our techs know where these fail. That knowledge points the search at the likeliest spots first.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Hickory

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Hickory

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Hickory, NC

    Under-slab drains crack as well. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, which is why we push a camera through to look for cracks, bellies and pulled joints. We show you the video before discussing any repair in your Hickory home.

    Listening Through Concrete in Hickory

    Ground microphones are sensitive. That means appliances, HVAC fans and traffic all show up in the headphones, so our techs shut off what they can and learn to tune out the rest, and that skill only comes with lots of practice. A pressurized line gives the clearest sound in your Hickory home.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Hickory, NC

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. It shrinks the search area before we listen.
