    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Hickory, NC

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Hickory

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. You get the video file. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Hickory

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Crawlspace foundations are very common here, which makes many plumbing repairs more accessible than they would be under a slab. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Hickory

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first. Our dispatcher will walk your staff to the shutoff over the phone if needed, then send a plumber who can isolate the branch, restore water to the rest of the building and make the permanent repair. Sewage backups get the same response. After clearing the line we run a camera so you know whether it is a one-time clog or damaged pipe.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Hickory, NC

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Running flush valves, stuck urinals and dripping sensor faucets usually need a diaphragm, a solenoid or fresh batteries. We stock those. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Hickory

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Hickory, NC

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless racks get descaled. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Hickory

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by. A sectional machine opens the line, and a jetter cleans grease off the walls of the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so it stays open longer. We also check trap primers.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Hickory

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. Passing devices get tagged. The provider gets the report from us, and we keep a copy for your next test.
