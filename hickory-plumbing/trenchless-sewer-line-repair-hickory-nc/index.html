    Pipe bursting uses high-density polyethylene, HDPE, fused into one continuous length with no joints. For lining, it is a felt or fiberglass tube saturated with epoxy resin.     A rented drum machine might clear the clog for a week or two, but it will not fix a crack, an offset or the roots that keep growing back through a broken joint. Running a cable through fragile pipe can tear it further. A camera inspection is the smarter first step.
