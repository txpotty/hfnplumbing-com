    Sewer Pipe Relining in Wilmington, NC

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Wilmington

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Wilmington

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first.     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Wilmington, NC

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.
