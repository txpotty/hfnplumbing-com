    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. Cables can kink, whip and wrap around a wrist in a second. Rentals jam in pipes. Getting a rental cable stuck in clay pipe can mean digging it out, which costs far more than a rooter visit.

    Watch for very slow drains, gurgling from toilets and sour odors. Water coming up in the shower when something else drains is a classic main line symptom. Act on that quickly. Waiting raises the odds that the next flush puts sewage on the floor.

    Clearing a clog in Wilmington does not need a permit. Repair work is different. If the camera shows broken pipe that needs a dig, a new cleanout or a sewer replacement, a plumbing permit is usually required. We handle the permit and the inspection.

    Gurgling toilets, sewage odors and slow drains in several fixtures are the common signs. Water backing up into a tub when you flush is another clue. That one is serious. Repeat clogs in one line usually mean a deeper problem, often roots at a joint well out in the yard.

    Snaking runs a spinning cable with a cutting head through the clog.     Chemicals sometimes open a small hole in a hair clog, but they will not touch roots or a grease plug. They can soften old PVC glue joints and pit cast iron over time.
