    Control Box & Electrical Repair in Wilmington, NC

    Emergency Well Pump Repair in Wilmington

    No water at all means a house that cannot run. HFN Plumbing handles emergency well pump calls in Wilmington ahead of routine work. Technicians carry gauges, an amp clamp and the usual switches and capacitors, so many no-water calls are fixed on the first trip. A full pump swap takes longer.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Wilmington

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Wilmington

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. We inspect the bladder, the switch and the tank connections. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Wilmington, NC

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. When they fault, the drive shows a code that guides diagnosis. The drive, sensor and wiring get tested, and the failed part gets repaired or replaced before we verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.
