    Main Sewer Line Hydro Jetting in Wilmington

    Every drain in the house empties into the main. A clog there stops them all. Mains are often hit by roots from large trees planted near the line, and jetting cuts and flushes those roots while clearing the built-up waste around them all the way to the city sewer.

    Hydro Jetting Cost Compared to Snaking in Wilmington, NC

    Jetting costs more on the first visit. The results hold up better. If you are paying for snaking several times a year, jetting often costs less over time because it removes the coating that causes the repeat clogs in the first place, though a simple soft clog may only need a cable.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Wilmington

    Cast iron grows rust tubercles. Minerals make it worse. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and restore flow, while we keep the pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots, which is also the standard prep before pipe lining.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydro Jetting in Wilmington

    Mature trees near old sewers cause trouble. They enter at joints and cracks. A root-cutting nozzle uses a powerful spinning stream to cut and flush out the roots, removing more of the fine growth than a cable and slowing how fast it grows back into the line.

    High-Pressure Water Jetting for Drain Cleaning in Wilmington

    Our jetters run up to 4,000 PSI. That breaks apart hard buildup. The same jet that clears grease from PVC can descale cast iron, as long as the pressure matches the pipe.

    Large-Line Commercial Jetting in Wilmington, NC

    Big buildings need trailer jetters. More flow, longer reach. Restaurants, food processors, hotels and apartment complexes create heavy grease and solids loads, and we clean interceptor outlets, floor drains and main laterals on a schedule that fits your operation, with written records for inspections.

    Hydro Jetting Before Pipe Lining in Wilmington, NC

    Liners need clean pipe. Dirt ruins the bond. We jet the host pipe to remove roots, scale and debris, then camera it to confirm it is ready, cutting out any heavy obstructions before the liner goes in, since skipping prep can lead to wrinkles or poor bonding.

    Scheduled Jetting for Repeat Clogs in Wilmington, NC

    Recurring clogs follow a pattern. Plan ahead and they stop. Jetting once or twice a year keeps mineral deposits, grease and sludge from building up again, which avoids emergency blockages and extends the life of the plumbing system on properties with a history of trouble.
