    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Wilmington

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. Hairline cracks sometimes take a plastic weld. Anything through the floor of the tank or along a molded seam we treat as a replacement, because patches there fail under the weight of a full trap.     Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Wilmington, NC

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling.     Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Wilmington

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead.     Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Wilmington, NC

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. On the inlet side, the tee directs incoming water down under the floating grease. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Wilmington

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Without it, a full sink dump pushes grease straight through. Inspectors look for it. We also clear the air intake, since a plugged intake on the flow control fitting lets the trap siphon.
