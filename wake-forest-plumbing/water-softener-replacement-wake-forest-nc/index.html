    Softener Systems We Install in Wake Forest, NC

    Fleck Valve Programming in Wake Forest, NC

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Wake Forest

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Wake Forest, NC

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Wake Forest

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Wake Forest, NC

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in North Carolina homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Wake Forest

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Use pellet salt.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Wake Forest, NC

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Obsolete valves are another story.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Wake Forest

    Not every softener failure is urgent. A unit leaking from the valve body or the brine tank overflowing onto the floor is, and we move those calls up the schedule. Turn the bypass handle and wait for us.
