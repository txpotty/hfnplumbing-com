    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Wake Forest

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Wake Forest

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Either fuel works. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Wake Forest, NC

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Then we check for leaks. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Wake Forest, NC

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Wake Forest, NC

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Wake Forest

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. Our crews install to spec. Your warranty stays intact.
