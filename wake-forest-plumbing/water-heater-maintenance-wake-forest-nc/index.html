    Tankless Water Heater Service in Wake Forest, NC

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. We flush it with descaler. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Tankless Descaling in Wake Forest, NC

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Municipal water in North Carolina is generally soft, but many private wells produce acidic water that can eat away at copper pipe over time. A pump runs descaler through the exchanger for the recommended time, followed by a clean water flush. We clean the inlet filter. Igniter, burner and venting get a safety check.

    Anode Rod Checks in Wake Forest

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. It comes out for inspection. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.

    Water Heater Problem Diagnosis in Wake Forest, NC

    Something sounds or feels wrong. Rumbling, short hot water or a wet pan get diagnosed on the spot, with a straight answer on repair or replace. You get options. The call is yours.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Wake Forest

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Wake Forest, NC

    Small drips become floods. Connections, the relief valve, the drain valve and the seams get checked, along with the pan and floor. Rust stains are a warning. Catching it early saves the floor.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Wake Forest

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. We check the pan and drain. Access platforms and venting get looked at too.

    Electric Element Service in Wake Forest, NC

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. We only replace what failed.
