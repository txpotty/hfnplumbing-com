    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Wake Forest

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. We replace spent media. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Wake Forest

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Wake Forest

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Noritz Descaling in Wake Forest, NC

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Wake Forest, NC

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Wake Forest, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. The manufacturer's steps come first, not a generic checklist. That matters for warranty claims down the road.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Wake Forest, NC

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. We flush the recirculation loop along with the exchanger. Next we confirm crossover valve or dedicated return, and set the pump schedule to fit.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Wake Forest

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Wake Forest

Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.
