    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Wake Forest, NC

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots.     Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Wake Forest

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank.
