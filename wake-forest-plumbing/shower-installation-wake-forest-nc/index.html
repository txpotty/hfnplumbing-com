    How We Install Showers in Wake Forest, NC

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Wake Forest

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Wake Forest

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. We phase the work to keep some showers running.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Wake Forest, NC

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. Thresholds stay low or flat. ADA layouts are available on request.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Wake Forest, NC

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. Good for rentals and guest baths. Usually a one-day job.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Wake Forest, NC

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement.     Curbless Shower Installation in Wake Forest

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. A linear drain often works best. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Wake Forest

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Steel and acrylic tubs need a mortar bed or foam under them, and cast iron needs a level ledger, so the tub sits flat and the caulk joint at the wall does not crack. The valve goes in next. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Wake Forest, NC

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.
