    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Wake Forest, NC

    The sound of running water with everything off is a textbook slab leak symptom. So is a hot spot on the floor.     Frozen Pipe Thawing and Repair in Wake Forest, NC

    Sudden cold snaps can catch North Carolina homeowners off guard, especially in homes with uninsulated crawlspace plumbing. Start by opening the faucet on that line. If the line already split, we cut out the damaged length and replace it, then suggest insulation or heat cable for that run.
