    What Goes Into a Repipe in Wake Forest, NC

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Wake Forest, NC

    Pinhole leaks start small. First a damp spot, then drywall damage. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Wake Forest

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    PEX Repiping in Wake Forest

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Wake Forest

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. Finding the exact spot comes first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, which skips the jackhammer. We explain both.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Wake Forest

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Work has to fit around your hours. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, keeping restrooms and kitchens running while we work on other sections. Night work available.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Wake Forest

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. Chlorine breaks it down over time. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Wake Forest, NC

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, so we avoid tearing open walls just to pull out dead pipe. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Wake Forest, NC

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Shut off the main valve first. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and we can usually get there, stop the leak and replace the failed section the same day. Drying comes next.
