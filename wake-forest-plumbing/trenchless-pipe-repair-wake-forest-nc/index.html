    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Wake Forest

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Wake Forest yard.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Wake Forest

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They hold up in tough conditions.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Wake Forest

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Pipe Bursting in Wake Forest, NC

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.
