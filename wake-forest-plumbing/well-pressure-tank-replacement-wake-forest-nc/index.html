    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Wake Forest

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Wake Forest, NC

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Wake Forest

    Big properties need big drawdown. Livestock and irrigation draw a lot. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, so heavy-use systems still keep the pump from short cycling. Floor space matters.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Wake Forest

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Wake Forest, NC

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Wake Forest, NC

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. We put in plenty of them. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, which helps the tank resist rust from the inside, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Wake Forest

    Rapid clicking at the switch is the classic sign. That fast on-off pattern means the tank has no usable air, and the pump is now taking every hit of demand directly, which wears the pump and the switch contacts. We swap the tank and the cycling stops.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Wake Forest

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. Replacing just the tank can expose other weak spots. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, so you can choose a tank-only swap or a fuller refresh. Your call.
