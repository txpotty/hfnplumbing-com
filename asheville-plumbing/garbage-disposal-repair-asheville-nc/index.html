    Disposal Repair in Asheville, NC: Symptom by Symptom

    Leak Detection and Repair in Asheville, NC

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Asheville

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Asheville

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Asheville

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Asheville, NC

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Most repairs use parts from the truck. A failed motor means a new unit.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Asheville

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Asheville

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually include a control box and a solenoid valve for water. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Asheville

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.
