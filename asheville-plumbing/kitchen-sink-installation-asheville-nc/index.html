    Top-mount Sink Installation in Asheville

    Drop-in sinks are common in North Carolina homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Asheville, NC

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. Deep bowls need a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Composite Sink Installation in Asheville

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Asheville

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. Usually the fastest upgrade. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Asheville

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Asheville, NC

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Vents go in now. Having the sink and faucet specs early saves moving pipe later.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Asheville, NC

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Asheville, NC

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.
