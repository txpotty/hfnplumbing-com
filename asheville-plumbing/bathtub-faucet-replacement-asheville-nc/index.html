    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Asheville, NC

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Asheville

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Asheville

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Asheville, NC

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Asheville, NC

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. Snapped handles and broken spouts get same-day visits when a plumber is free. Shut off the water first. Leave the tub drain open.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Asheville

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Asheville
