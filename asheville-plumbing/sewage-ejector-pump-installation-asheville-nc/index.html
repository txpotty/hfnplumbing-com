    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Asheville

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Flooding can overwhelm municipal sewers and saturate septic drain fields, causing slow drains and backups inside the home. Battery backup is an option.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Asheville

    Residential ejector systems in Asheville serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Asheville, NC

    Businesses in Asheville, NC with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house.     Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Asheville, NC

    The basin holds everything, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. A larger basin means longer, less frequent cycles that spare the motor and switch, so we go bigger when the pit takes a laundry or several fixtures. Every lid gets sealed.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Asheville, NC
