    Softener Systems We Replace in Asheville, NC

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Asheville, NC

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. The valve's age decides it. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Asheville

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Softener Loop Installation in Asheville, NC

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Asheville, NC

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Asheville

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Asheville

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Asheville

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Or the whole system.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Asheville

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    A local plumber can also check how the softener ties into your water heater and main line. We spot loop leaks and drain problems that cut performance, like a drain line with no air gap or a bypass valve that was left half open. Quotes are straightforward.
