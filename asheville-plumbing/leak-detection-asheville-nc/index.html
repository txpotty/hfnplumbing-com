    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Asheville

    A soft wet patch near the sidewalk or a sinking spot over the line can mean the service pipe is leaking. Old galvanized and copper lines fail most. We map the pipe with a line tracer and listen for the leak with a ground microphone, so excavation stays to one small hole instead of a trench across the yard. Repair options follow.

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Asheville

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Crawlspace foundations are very common here, which makes many plumbing repairs more accessible than they would be under a slab. We isolate the line first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak so nobody has to break the floor open to search, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before the saw comes out.

    Leak Testing for Multifamily and Offices in Asheville

    Multifamily buildings and office parks have long supply systems that let leaks hide for months. We isolate each unit or floor, test it and trace leaks with the same acoustic and thermal tools we use in single-family houses. Every unit gets its own report. Catching leaks early cuts tenant complaints and insurance claims across the building.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Asheville

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, which makes it tricky to separate evaporation from a real leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, since a leak left alone can wash out the soil behind the shell.

    Emergency Leak Detection in Asheville, NC

    Water coming through a ceiling at night does not wait for an appointment. Shut the main. We respond to Asheville, NC emergencies with full detection gear, isolate the failed line and restore water to the rest of the house while the permanent repair gets scheduled.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Asheville, NC

    A yearly leak check catches small problems early. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors get the most out of this, because those parts often fail together. Short visit, big savings.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Asheville

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.
