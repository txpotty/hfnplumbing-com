    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Asheville

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Asheville

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. When the body is corroded or the brand stopped making cartridges for it, we replace the valve, usually through a small access panel behind the shower wall so the tile stays put. New valves include pressure balancing. Thermostatic valves are an option if you want exact temperature control.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Asheville

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Asheville, NC

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. Both ride on our trucks. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Asheville

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Asheville

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Asheville, NC

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Asheville

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Shut the main valve, catch the drip in a bucket and keep people out from under any sagging drywall, since a soaked ceiling can let go without warning. Then give us a call. We find the source, stop it and dry out what we can that day.
