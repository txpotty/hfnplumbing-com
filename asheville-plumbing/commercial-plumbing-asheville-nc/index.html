    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Asheville, NC

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. Our work covers high-input tanks, multi-unit tankless racks and boilers feeding indirect storage tanks. Municipal water in North Carolina is generally soft, but many private wells produce acidic water that can eat away at copper pipe over time. Scale cuts output fast. We set up a flushing and descaling schedule, and we install thermostatic mixing valves so hand sinks deliver tempered water without scalding anyone.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Asheville

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. We tag every passing device. The provider gets the report from us, and we keep a copy for your next test.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Asheville

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Asheville

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. We also check trap primers.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Asheville

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Dense red clay is common in central parts of North Carolina, and it holds water and shifts with the seasons, stressing buried pipes. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. We price both so you can compare.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Asheville, NC

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Running toilets count too. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Grease Trap Installation in Asheville

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Asheville

Our testers handle RPZ, double check and PVB devices with annually calibrated gauges and submit results the way your provider wants them. If a device fails, we usually rebuild it the same day.
