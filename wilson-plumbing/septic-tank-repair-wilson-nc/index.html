    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Wilson, NC

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Wilson

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. We measure sludge and scum on every maintenance visit, and we pump when the solids reach about a third of the tank depth. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Wilson

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. Split tanks get replaced.

    Septic Tee Repair in Wilson

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet can crack or get knocked off by the hose on a pump truck. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.
