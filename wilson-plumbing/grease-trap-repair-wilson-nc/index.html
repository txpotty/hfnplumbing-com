    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Wilson, NC

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Hydrogen sulfide from the waste eats the concrete above the waterline over time. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Nobody goes in without a gas monitor, a tripod and a second person topside.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Wilson, NC

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. Once a steel unit has rust holes in the body, it is finished. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. We put both options in writing with a price on each.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Wilson

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Wilson

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Wilson, NC

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. A cracked riser lets surface water and grit into the interceptor. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. Pedestrian lids in a drive lane are a liability.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Wilson, NC

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. Once cracked, it leaks smell out and lets mop water into the trap. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. Baffle damage often shows up at the same visit.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Wilson, NC

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. Grease past the outlet is a red flag. We jet the downstream run to the property line or the sewer tap, then camera it to confirm the pipe is clear and to document any cracked or sagging sections for a repair quote. If we only clear the line and skip the trap, it clogs again.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Wilson, NC

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Steel trap bodies also rust through at the bottom seams after enough years of hot, acidic water. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Some days the fix is a cheap gasket and ten minutes. If the seam itself has opened, patching rarely holds and we will tell you so before you pay for it.
