    How We Set New Sinks in Wilson, NC

    Undermount Sink Installation in Wilson

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Wilson, NC

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Wilson, NC

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Wilson

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. Swapping a chipped drop-in for a new one in the same cutout usually takes a morning, and moving up to a deeper single bowl may only need a new trap and a short drain extension. Countertop changes add time. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Wilson

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. A cast iron farmhouse bowl can top a hundred pounds empty, so the base cabinet often gets reinforced before it goes in. We check the cabinet before the sink arrives. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in Wilson

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. Wall-hung or countertop lavatories go on carriers with sensor faucets and mixing valves where tempered water is required. We can fit tamper-proof parts. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Wilson, NC

    New sinks often get a new disposal. The disposal goes on a new flange, the dishwasher knockout gets punched, and the outlet lines up above the trap. Forgetting the knockout plug floods the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Wilson, NC

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.
