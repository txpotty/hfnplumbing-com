    Electric Water Heater Installation in Wilson, NC

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. They do not need a vent. You get more spots to put it.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Wilson

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. Downtime costs you money. Night installs are available.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Wilson

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Wilson, NC

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through.     Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Wilson, NC

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Wilson, NC

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We help you decide if one fits.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Wilson

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Wilson, NC

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.
