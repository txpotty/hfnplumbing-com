    Repiping Services We Handle in Wilson, NC

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Wilson, NC

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Wilson, NC

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Wilson

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Wilson, NC

    Well owners around Wilson benefit from periodic water testing and, when needed, neutralizers or filters that protect pipes and appliances. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Wilson

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Wilson

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. It suits a first-time leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.

    Copper Repiping in Wilson, NC

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Copper takes a bit longer. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Drain Line Repiping in Wilson

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.
