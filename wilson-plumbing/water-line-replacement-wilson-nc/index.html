    Copper Service Line Installation in Wilson, NC

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record.     Copper Water Line Installation in Wilson

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. It handles high street pressure without complaint. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe. PEX is cheaper.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Wilson, NC

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, meeting NSF 61.
