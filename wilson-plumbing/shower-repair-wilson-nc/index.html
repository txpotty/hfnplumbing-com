    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Wilson, NC

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open.     Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Wilson, NC

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. For a sudden wall leak or a valve that will not close, the main valve near the meter is the first move. Then call us. We stop the leak, make the repair or a safe temporary fix and check for anything that needs drying.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Wilson

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Wilson

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Wilson, NC

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Wilson, NC

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Wilson

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Wilson, NC

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.
