    Main Water Line Repair in Wilson

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. A leak here shows up as a soggy strip of lawn and a meter dial that never rests. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Wilson, NC

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. You see the cut-out section yourself.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Wilson, NC

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. A large share of homes across the state rely on private wells, and iron and manganese often leave rusty or dark stains on fixtures. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Wilson, NC

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. We shut it down first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, and if the repair will run past a few hours we set a temporary bypass to the house. Most breaks are fixed the same day. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Wilson, NC

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Repair in Wilson

    Spot repairs on an accessible line usually run $350 to $1,500. Depth drives most of that. The written price comes before we start.

    Small spot repairs often do not need one. A new service line almost always does. Rules vary by jurisdiction in Wilson, so HFN Plumbing checks the local requirement for your address and pulls the permit when one applies. The inspector sees the trench before it is backfilled.

    Often the pipe itself is not covered. Resulting damage sometimes is.     We dig a pit at each end of the old line. First, a cable is threaded through. A bursting head on the cable splits the old line outward, and new HDPE pipe follows right behind it into the same space. It only works when the old pipe is a fairly straight run, and most home service lines qualify.

    In most places the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. The homeowner owns the line to the house.     Most jurisdictions in North Carolina require a permit for a full service line replacement. Spot repairs vary.     Look for a wet or extra-green patch in the yard. Listen at the meter box for hissing. Low pressure at every fixture, rusty water and a climbing bill are other common clues. The surest check is your meter. A moving dial means water is escaping somewhere.
