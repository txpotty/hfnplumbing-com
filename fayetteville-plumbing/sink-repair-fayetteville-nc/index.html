    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Fayetteville, NC

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Fayetteville, NC

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Fayetteville

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. If the angle stop will not stop it, the main shutoff will, and the sooner it is closed the less water ends up inside the vanity floor and the subfloor below. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Fayetteville

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Fayetteville

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Fayetteville

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Fayetteville

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Fayetteville, NC

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. A trap or stopper costs a fraction of a new sink, and replacement only makes sense when the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain body has rusted into the basin. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.
