    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Fayetteville, NC

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Fayetteville, NC homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current.     Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Fayetteville

    Propane homes in Fayetteville are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Propane runs at a higher pressure than natural gas, so the regulator, gas line sizing and the unit's gas valve all have to match, and the propane supplier may need to change the regulator at the tank.     Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Fayetteville

    Gas tankless units in Fayetteville are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. Gas is the catch. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Fayetteville, NC

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Fayetteville

    Conversions in Fayetteville usually take a full day. We drain and remove the tank, upsize and test the gas, run a new vent through a wall or roof, then mount, plumb and fire the unit with service valves. You shower that night. The inspector comes after.

We plan both on the quote.
