    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Fayetteville, NC

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Fayetteville, NC

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. HFN Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. You get a clear breakdown.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Fayetteville, NC

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Fayetteville
