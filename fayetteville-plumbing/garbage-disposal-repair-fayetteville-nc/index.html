    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems.     Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    When a disposal fails, water can back into the sink and the dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We respond fast.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Fayetteville

    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller.
