    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Fayetteville

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. With hydro jetting added, expect roughly $450 to $1,000, depending on how long the line is and how heavy the grease is. Nights and weekends run higher.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting works another way. A jetter scours the walls with water at high pressure. Many tough lines do best with both, cut first to open the line and jetted second to wash out what the blades left.

    Slow drains, gurgling pipes and sewer smell are the early ones. If the tub fills when the toilet flushes, the main is blocked. Do not wait on that one. Several fixtures acting up at once almost always points to a main line clog rather than a single trap under one sink.

    We run RIDGID, Spartan and Gorlitz augers, plus sewer cameras and jetters from the same tier of commercial makers. They survive heavy daily use. Brand matters less than setup. Even a top machine can hurt a pipe with the wrong head, so picking the cutter gets as much attention as the machine.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. Main lines with heavy roots, or jobs that need a jetting pass, can stretch to half a day or more. Access is the wild card. A buried or missing cleanout means pulling a toilet first, which adds about an hour.

    Every rooter job from HFN Plumbing carries a guarantee for a set period on the line we cleared. The terms are explained before work begins. It covers the line we worked on. If that same line stops up again within the guarantee window, we come back and clear it at no extra charge.
