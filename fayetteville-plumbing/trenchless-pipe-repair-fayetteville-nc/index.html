    Pipe Bursting Services in Fayetteville, NC

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Fayetteville, NC soil.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Fayetteville

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. We burst or bore.     Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Crawlspace foundations are very common here, which makes many plumbing repairs more accessible than they would be under a slab.     Sectional Point Repair in Fayetteville

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Fayetteville sewer lines.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Fayetteville

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. Good prep makes a good liner.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Fayetteville

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Fayetteville yard.
