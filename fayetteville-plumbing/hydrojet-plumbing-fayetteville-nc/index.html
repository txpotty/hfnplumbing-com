    Residential Hydrojetting in Fayetteville

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. We set up outside at the cleanout whenever we can, so the jetter hose never has to come through your house or across your floors. Kitchen lines may need access under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Fayetteville

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Fayetteville, NC

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Fayetteville, NC

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Flooding can back up municipal sewers and saturate septic drain fields, causing slow drains and backups inside the home. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Fayetteville

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Fayetteville

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Fayetteville, NC

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Multifamily and office buildings carry heavy daily loads. Commercial stacks and mains get jetted outside business hours, with a written report for the manager.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Fayetteville, NC

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.
