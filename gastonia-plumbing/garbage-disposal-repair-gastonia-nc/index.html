    Commercial Disposal Repair in Gastonia

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Gastonia

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Most smells start at the splash guard.     Leak Detection and Repair in Gastonia, NC

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Gastonia

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.
