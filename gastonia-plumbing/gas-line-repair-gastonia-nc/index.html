    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Gastonia

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Gastonia

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Gastonia building officials.     Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Gastonia, NC

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it.     CSST Repair and Bonding in Gastonia, NC

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out.
