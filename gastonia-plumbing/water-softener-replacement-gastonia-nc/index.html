    Commercial Softener Replacement in Gastonia

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. Sizing comes from peak flow and daily use. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Gastonia

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Gastonia

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Gastonia

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Gastonia, NC

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. The valve's age decides it. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Gastonia, NC

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. A basic 32,000-grain metered unit costs far less than a twin-tank Kinetico, and for a small household on moderately hard water it may be all you need. We show both options side by side.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Gastonia

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. Installs happen before opening or after close.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Gastonia, NC

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.
