    Hot Water Tank Service in Gastonia, NC

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Gastonia

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Gastonia

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. Sediment drain, anode check, burner or element inspection and a relief valve test cover both tank and tankless. Most failures give warning. A yearly tune-up catches the warning signs.

    Water Softener Service in Gastonia

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.
