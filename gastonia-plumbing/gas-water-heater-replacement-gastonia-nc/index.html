    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Gastonia, NC

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. We price it against how long you plan to stay.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Gastonia

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Gastonia

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of North Carolina plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Gastonia, NC

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most North Carolina homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We check clearance before we order. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.
