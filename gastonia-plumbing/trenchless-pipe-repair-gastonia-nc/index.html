    Lateral and Spot Lining in Gastonia

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. A few bad joints need a short fix. A sectional liner is cheaper and quicker for a short stretch of damage, though we camera the rest of the line first to be sure it is sound.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Gastonia

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Gastonia yard.

    Commercial Trenchless Solutions in Gastonia

    Commercial sites in Gastonia have longer runs and more laterals. Pavement covers most of it. Schools, offices and stores keep lots and walkways open because we work from cleanouts and manholes, cutting only small pits. We schedule around your hours.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Gastonia, NC

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Gastonia, NC soil.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Gastonia

    Older Gastonia homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.
