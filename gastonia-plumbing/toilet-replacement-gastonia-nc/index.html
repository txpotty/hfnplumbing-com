    What a Toilet Replacement Covers in Gastonia, NC

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Gastonia

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. No plumbing changes are needed. Many people pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Gastonia, NC

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. Bowls are matched to the flush valve's gallons per flush, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Gastonia, NC

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Closing the angle stop stops the water. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Gastonia, NC

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Gastonia

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Gastonia

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Gastonia

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Battery and hardwired models are both available. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Gastonia, NC

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.
