    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Gastonia

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Gastonia

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Gastonia

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Gastonia, NC

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Gastonia

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Gastonia

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Gastonia

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Gastonia, NC

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. We flush the recirculation loop along with the exchanger. Then we check whether the system uses a crossover valve or a dedicated return line and set the schedule to match.
