    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan.     Preventive Maintenance Checks in Gastonia, NC

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems.
