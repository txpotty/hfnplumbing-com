    Water Softener Replacement in Gastonia, NC

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Gastonia

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. Several models are available.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Gastonia

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Gastonia, NC

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Gastonia, NC

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Gastonia

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Gastonia

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Gastonia

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Gastonia

    Most installs cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. A new loop typically adds $500 to $1,500 based on access to the main.

    With a loop in place, some homeowners can do it, but air gaps, bypass valves and programming matter. Hiring a plumber avoids leaks and poor results.

    On an existing loop, installs typically take two or three hours. A new loop adds a few hours.

    A good softener usually lasts ten to fifteen years. Unit quality, how hard your water is and how well the system is maintained all change that, and resin can be replaced to extend it. Service helps.

    A softener targets calcium and magnesium to stop scale. Filters handle chlorine, sediment and taste, and many homes pair the two.

    Softeners with Fleck and Clack control valves are what we install most. Our picks favor good performance and easy service.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. A pressure drop after install usually means the valve is too small or a filter is clogged.
