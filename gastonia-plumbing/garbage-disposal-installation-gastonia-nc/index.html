    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Gastonia, NC

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Gastonia

    For kitchens that have never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. We install the mount, hang the disposal and connect the discharge tube to a new trap, lowering the drain at the wall if the old stub sits too high for the disposal outlet. An electrician adds power if needed.

    Commercial Disposal Installation in Gastonia

    Commercial disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and typically come with a control panel and solenoid valve and need their own circuit. We set the unit on the mounting collar, run the drain to the grease trap where the local rules require it, and wire the controls so the water runs whenever the motor does. Local rules differ. We check the requirement before we order anything.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Gastonia

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.

    Disposal Installation in Rentals in Gastonia

    Landlords want disposals that survive tenants. Our picks for rentals are durable models with easy mounts and decent warranties, and we can do multiple units in one trip during turnover. Fast installs shorten turnover. We leave a care sheet for the tenant.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Gastonia, NC

    A 1 HP disposal earns its keep in a busy kitchen, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model.
