    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Gastonia

    Clearing a clog usually runs $150 to $400. Structural fixes are higher. Main line repairs can be anywhere from $800 to $5,000 or more, and excavation pushes the price toward the top of that range. The written quote comes after diagnosis.

    A trap swap or a fixture drain fix is usually an hour or two. Main lines take longer. Replacing a section of main line can take a full day or several days, with permits and digging as the main things that stretch it out. The schedule is in the quote.

    We install PVC and ABS for most new drain work. Some jobs call for cast iron. Cast iron still makes sense in some multi-story walls where noise matters, and we repair existing copper, galvanized and cast iron drains with materials that match local code. Every part meets code.

    A dry trap is the most common cause. Run water in unused drains. A cracked drain fitting, a failed wax ring under the toilet or a blocked vent can also let sewer gas into the room, and we track it down with a smoke test or a close visual check. The fix is often quick.

    Snugging a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is a fair DIY job. Hand-tight plus a quarter turn.     PVC, ABS, cast iron, galvanized and copper drain all show up in North Carolina homes. We repair every one. Joining new plastic to old metal takes a shielded transition coupling sized to the outside diameter of each pipe, and getting that fitting right is what keeps the repair from leaking later. The common sizes ride on the truck.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair changes the pipe itself. We check on video.

    Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Repairs run longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack can take a full day or more, depending on access and whether a permit inspection is needed. We give you a timeline with the price.
