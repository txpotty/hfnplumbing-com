    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Gastonia

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Gastonia

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Gastonia

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Stop using water first. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Gastonia, NC
