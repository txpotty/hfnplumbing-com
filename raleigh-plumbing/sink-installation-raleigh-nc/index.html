    How We Set New Sinks in Raleigh, NC

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Raleigh, NC

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Raleigh

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Raleigh

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in Raleigh, NC

    Labor is the main cost driver. Moving the drain or adding a second bowl adds hours, and a straight drop-in swap is the cheapest. We quote labor apart from the sink. You can buy it or we can.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Raleigh

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Raleigh, NC

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. Undermounts cost more for clips and cure time, and vessels add a special drain and a taller faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Raleigh

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. A same-size drop-in swap usually takes a morning, and a deeper bowl may only need a new trap. New counters add a day. We tell you which one you are looking at.
