    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Raleigh

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it.     Black Iron Pipe Repair in Raleigh, NC

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Raleigh

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Raleigh building officials. We file the permit, schedule the inspection and are on site when the inspector checks the work and the test gauge. Inspectors often come within the week. After it passes, the gas company can restore service, and we can return to relight the pilots.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Raleigh, NC

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Raleigh, NC

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. Sizing is the first thing we check. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Cost of Gas Line Repair in Raleigh, NC

    Gas line repair prices in Raleigh, NC depend on where the leak is and how much pipe has to change. Tightening or replacing a single fitting or connector is usually a few hundred dollars. Replacing a buried line that needs trenching across the yard can run $1,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on length, digging conditions and whether the driveway or a patio is in the way. We put the price in writing first.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Raleigh

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Coastal areas have sandy soil and high water tables, which affect how sewer lines and septic systems perform. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel.
