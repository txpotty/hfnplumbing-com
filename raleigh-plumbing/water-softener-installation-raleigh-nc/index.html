    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Raleigh

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Raleigh

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. That keeps salt use reasonable. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Raleigh

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Raleigh

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Raleigh, NC

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HFN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Raleigh

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Raleigh, NC

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Raleigh, NC

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.
