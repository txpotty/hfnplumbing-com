    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Raleigh, NC

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Replacement costs thousands. A sound pipe is worth maintaining, while a collapsed or sagging one may need repair, and the camera tells us which, and you see both prices.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Raleigh

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Joints are the weak spot. We clear root growth with cutters and jetting and talk about lining or repair to stop the next round.

    Large Commercial Laterals in Raleigh, NC

    Big commercial laterals carry more solids. Kitchens add grease. We use trailer jetters with higher flow to clean 4, 6 and 8 inch lines, with extra attention on grease interceptor outlets and long runs to the city main, plus written reports for maintenance logs.

    Urgent Sewer Backups in Raleigh

    Raw sewage in the house is an emergency. We treat it that way. Our technicians respond quickly with the equipment to clear severe blockages that pose a health hazard and threaten property damage, then help with cleanup.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Raleigh, NC

    Roots are the top cause of blockages in established neighborhoods. Waste catches on them. After cutting the mass with a root saw, we jet the line to clear root hairs and leave the pipe clean, then show each entry point on camera.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Raleigh, NC

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. A snake punches a hole. High-pressure water strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes them toward the street, and with the right settings it is safe for PVC and older clay alike.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Raleigh

    Scheduled cleaning beats emergency calls. Roots grow back. A regular cleaning schedule keeps roots and grease in check, especially on older lines near big trees.
