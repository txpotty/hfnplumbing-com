    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Raleigh, NC

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, which is why we ask which fixtures are acting up before we decide where to open the line. The right entry point saves time.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Raleigh, NC

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Not even close. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they form a dense plug that a cable can poke a hole through but not fully remove from the pipe. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Toss wipes in the trash.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Raleigh

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That is the difference. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Many lines stay clear for years after.

    Tree Root Removal Services in Raleigh

    Older Raleigh homes with mature trees see more root clogs than anyone. Old joints let them in. HFN Plumbing cuts roots out with a sectional machine and a root saw sized to the pipe, then jets the line to wash out the cut roots. Root treatment can slow regrowth between visits.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Raleigh, NC

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Cracked pipe is different. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, so the camera footage decides whether you spend a few hundred dollars on cleaning or more on a spot fix, liner or replacement. We walk you through it.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Raleigh

    Some older homes have no cleanout at all. Each clog costs more without one. Without one, a plumber has to pull a toilet or climb on the roof to reach the main, so we install a two-way cleanout near the foundation that gives direct access upstream and downstream. It pays for itself on the next call.
