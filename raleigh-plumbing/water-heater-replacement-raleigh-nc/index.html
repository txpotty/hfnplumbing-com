    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Raleigh, NC

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in Raleigh, NC. Tankless and hybrid units cost more up front but can lower operating costs, and tankless often lasts 15 to 20 years versus about 8 to 12 for a tank. We price all three.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Raleigh

    Older homes around Raleigh may have galvanized steel supply lines or cast iron drains that are wearing out. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. We bring the help.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Raleigh, NC

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Raleigh, NC

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on the majority of our swaps. Bradford White is sold only through plumbers and known for durability, Rheem has a wide lineup and good parts support, and AO Smith offers many sizes and efficiency options, so the right pick usually depends on fit, warranty and stock. All three hold up.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Raleigh

    Electric water heaters in Raleigh usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Hot water is back the same day.
