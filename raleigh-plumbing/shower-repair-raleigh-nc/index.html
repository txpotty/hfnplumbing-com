    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Raleigh, NC

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Raleigh

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. We carry the common parts. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Raleigh, NC

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. Commercial valves get heavy-duty cartridges, and we schedule around your busy hours so the showers stay open when people need them. We stock tamper-resistant heads. Every repair is set to scald-limit code.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Raleigh, NC

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Raleigh, NC

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Raleigh, NC

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Raleigh, NC

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. We start with the head. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.
