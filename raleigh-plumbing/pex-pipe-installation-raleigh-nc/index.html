    What Goes Into a PEX Install in Raleigh, NC

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Raleigh

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Raleigh

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. Repiping does fix it. We replace every supply line from the main valve to each angle stop and test the system before closing anything.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Raleigh, NC

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Raleigh

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. A large share of homes across the state rely on private wells, and iron and manganese often leave orange or dark stains on fixtures. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Raleigh, NC

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Drywall patching may be separate. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Raleigh

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. A shower valve set two inches too low or a vanity stub that misses the cabinet back means opening the wall again after the tile is up, which is expensive and slow. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. The test happens before drywall goes up.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Raleigh

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating loops need oxygen-barrier PEX, which has a coating that keeps oxygen from migrating through the wall and rusting the boiler, the pump and any iron in the system. Loops have length limits. Run them too long and the last stretch never warms up.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Raleigh, NC

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. With the studs bare, we can run PEX back to a manifold or to the nearest good trunk, set new stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a line for the fridge ice maker. An addition gets sized on its own. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.
