    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Raleigh

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Raleigh, NC

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. The tank gets emptied, scrubbed and rinsed. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Resin Rebedding in Raleigh

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Repair or Replace a Softener in Raleigh, NC

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. If the tank and valve are in good shape, keeping them makes sense. We will tell you plainly to replace it when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Commercial Softener Service in Raleigh

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so a twin-tank system keeps one tank online while the other recharges. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Raleigh, NC

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Raleigh, NC

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 if they are worn.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Raleigh, NC

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.
