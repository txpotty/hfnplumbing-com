    Sump Pump Types We Install in Raleigh, NC

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Raleigh

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Raleigh

    Hurricanes and tropical storms can bring heavy rain and flooding to North Carolina, especially in coastal and low-lying areas. A standard pump just sits there once the power drops. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Raleigh, NC

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. The tech brings a replacement pump and a new check valve and can have water moving out of the pit within an hour of arrival in most cases. After that, we look at why the old one quit.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Raleigh

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Figure on an hour or two for a typical swap. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Raleigh, NC

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Check the breaker and the GFCI outlet first, then lift the lid and see whether the float is stuck. A shop vac or a bucket can hold the level down. We stock the common pumps on the truck, and most emergency swaps are done on the first trip.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Raleigh

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. From there it is math. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Raleigh, NC

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. We break out the concrete, set a larger poly basin with a sealed lid, and pour the floor back. More volume in the pit means fewer starts per hour. Fewer starts mean the motor lasts longer.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Raleigh, NC

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. Discharge goes away from the foundation. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.
