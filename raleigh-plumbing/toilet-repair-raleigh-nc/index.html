    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.

    Toilet Leaking Through the Ceiling in Raleigh, NC

    Water coming through the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom usually traces back to a failed wax ring or a cracked supply nut, and the drywall below holds the evidence. Stop using that toilet. The toilet comes up, the flange gets checked, and the bowl goes back on a new seal, then we show you how far the water traveled so you can decide about the ceiling.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Raleigh

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall.     Commercial Toilet Repair in Raleigh

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Commercial bowls usually run on flushometer valves rather than tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. Work can be scheduled before opening or after close.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Raleigh

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. You will not find most of these parts at a hardware store. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low line pressure gives you a weak, half-hearted flush.
