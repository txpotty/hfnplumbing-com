    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Raleigh

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Raleigh, NC

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Your city may want a permit. We check that first.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Raleigh

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Each brand uses its own cartridge, and in busy restrooms it may need changing every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Raleigh, NC

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support.     Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Raleigh

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Raleigh

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Corroded hanger bolts and a flush valve seized to the spud mean we come prepared to cut, and we protect the wall tile while we do it so the new fixture does not sit against broken edges. We never reuse the old gasket. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Raleigh

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. We install Sloan Royal or Zurn AquaFlush valves on a 3/4 inch supply, adjust the control stop for proper flow, and set the vacuum breaker the right distance above the bowl so it protects the drinking water. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Raleigh, NC

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.
