    Water Softener Repair in Raleigh, NC

    Municipal water in North Carolina is generally soft, but many private wells produce acidic water that can corrode copper pipe over time.     Drain and Pipe Repair in Raleigh

    Hydro-jetting at around 4,000 psi clears grease and scale that a cable snake only pokes a small hole through, which is why the clog keeps coming back. HFN Plumbing follows every jetting job with a camera so you can see whether the pipe is sound. Cracks and offset joints get repaired by section in copper, PEX, PVC or ABS. A broken pipe needs a repair, not more jetting.

    Water Leak Repair in Raleigh

    Hidden leaks sit inside walls, under sink cabinets and in the buried yard line that runs between the meter and the house. A thermal camera narrows it down.     Slow Drain Repair in Raleigh

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a plugged roof vent or a sagging drain line. We clear the line, camera it and repair the damaged section. Common fixes include replacing corroded cast iron, cutting out failed ABS joints, rebuilding a broken trap arm and resetting toilets on a new wax ring and flange. No monthly snaking.

    Gas and Electric Water Heater Repair in Raleigh

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. HFN Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. Tank calls usually come down to the thermocouple, the gas valve, an element or the anode. Tankless trouble is usually scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet screen, and a descaling flush fixes more of them than new parts do.
