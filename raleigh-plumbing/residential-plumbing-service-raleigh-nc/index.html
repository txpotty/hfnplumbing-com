    Pipe Repair and Repiping in Raleigh, NC

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. A single leak gets a clean copper splice or a PEX transition. Repeat leaks mean a repipe. A pipe split by a hard freeze gets cut out and replaced, then pressure-tested.

    Water Heater Repair and Installation in Raleigh

    A water heater works hardest in winter, when incoming water is coldest. We work on gas, electric, tank and tankless heaters from Rheem and Bradford White, and the usual culprits are a heavy layer of sediment or a thermocouple that has quit reading the pilot. No hot water is often a quick part swap. New units get an expansion tank and pan when the code requires it.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Raleigh, NC

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Raleigh

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Coastal areas have sandy soil and high water tables, which affect how sewer lines and septic systems perform. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. After that, you choose the repair. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Raleigh

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. Our plumbers swap failing pumps for cast iron submersibles and replace stuck floats and check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. You can test yours with a five-gallon bucket of water.

    Drain Cleaning and Clog Clearing in Raleigh, NC

    Grease is the big one in kitchen lines. A camera tells us if roots or a broken joint are behind a repeat clog.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Clogs in Raleigh

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line.
