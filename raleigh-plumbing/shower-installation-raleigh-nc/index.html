    How We Install Showers in Raleigh, NC

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Raleigh, NC

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. We work with your tile setter on the waterproofing membrane and the slope, since water has to reach the drain before it can roll out the open side of the shower. Mountain areas see much colder winters than the coast, so plumbing needs differ quite a bit from one end of the state to the other. Pan material follows from that.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Raleigh, NC

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. Each valve is set to the depth its trim requires, and we include integral stops whenever the valve supports them. Trim goes on after tile. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Raleigh

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. The water heater gets sized up. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Raleigh

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Older neighborhoods may have lower pressure. We test static pressure before designing the layout.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Raleigh

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Shower Pan Installation in Raleigh

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Raleigh

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Raleigh

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. We phase the work to keep some showers running.
