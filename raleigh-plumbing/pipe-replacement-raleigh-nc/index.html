    What Goes Into a Repipe in Raleigh, NC

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Raleigh

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. Finding the exact spot comes first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, and rerouting often costs less and avoids future slab leaks on that line. We explain both.

    PEX Repiping in Raleigh

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Raleigh

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Well owners around Raleigh benefit from periodic water testing and, when needed, neutralizers or filters that preserve pipes and appliances. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Raleigh

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. That pipe is worn out. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, and fixes pressure and water color at once. Worth it.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Raleigh, NC

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Raleigh, NC

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Shut off the main valve first. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and our crews aim to stop it fast. Drying comes next.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Raleigh, NC

    Pinhole leaks start small. First a damp spot, then drywall damage. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Raleigh, NC

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.
