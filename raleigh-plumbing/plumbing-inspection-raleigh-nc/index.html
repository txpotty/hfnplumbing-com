    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging.     Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Raleigh

    Standard plumbing inspections usually cost $150 to $300. Adding a sewer camera inspection usually brings the total to $250 to $500.

    High water bills, repeat clogs, slow drains and discolored water are common signs. Banging pipes count too. Strange sounds from the pipes or the water heater, along with visible moisture or mold near fixtures, usually point to an underlying problem that needs a professional look before it gets worse.

    Most homes do well with an inspection every one to two years. Buying a home is another time. If you are purchasing a property, live in an older house or have had recent leaks or backups, a yearly check gives you a better chance of catching the next problem early.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Gurgling drains count too. Damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell under a sink or a warm patch of tile can all point to a hidden leak that an inspection will find before it does more damage.

    Every one to two years works for most homes. Some houses need it yearly. If your home has original supply pipe, large trees near the sewer or a history of leaks, a yearly inspection catches problems while they are still small and cheap to fix.

    A standard home inspection takes about one and a half to three hours. Sewer video adds time. Larger homes, long sewer lines or a hidden leak that needs tracing can push it longer, and we will tell you the expected time when you book so you can plan around it.

    For an older home, it usually pays for itself. Age brings hidden issues. Older homes with galvanized pipe, old sewers or original heaters often hide problems, and an inspection lets you plan instead of react.

    No permit is needed for an inspection. Permits apply to the work. If the inspection leads to repairs or replacements that need a permit, we will say so up front and handle it if we do the job.
