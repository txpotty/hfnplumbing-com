    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Raleigh, NC

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Raleigh, NC

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Raleigh

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. Downtime costs you money. Installs can happen after close.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Raleigh

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs.     Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Raleigh

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it.     Gas Water Heater Installation in Raleigh, NC

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour.
