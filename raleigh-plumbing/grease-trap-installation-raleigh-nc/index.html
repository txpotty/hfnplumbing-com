    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Raleigh, NC

    Buffets, grocery delis and kitchens doing several hundred covers a night usually end up with an in-ground gravity interceptor of 1,000 gallons or more. We excavate, set the tank level on compacted gravel, and tie in the inlet and outlet. Coastal areas have sandy soil and high water tables, which affect how sewer lines and septic systems perform. We plan the dig and the backfill around what the soil report and the open hole actually show us. Risers bring the lids to grade.

    Restaurant Grease Trap Compliance in Raleigh, NC

    Most sewer authorities run a FOG program with an inspector who opens your trap and checks your pump-out manifests against the schedule they set. Fail that and you can face fines or a surcharge on your sewer bill. We install to pass. That means a sample port where the program wants one and a unit sized to the number their formula gives, not the number the last contractor guessed.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Raleigh, NC

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Raleigh, NC

    Commissary kitchens see a dozen trucks dumping wash water in the same two hours, which is a different load from a restaurant spreading it over a long shift. We size for that spike.     New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Raleigh

    Zurn Grease Trap Installation in Raleigh

    Zurn's indoor grease interceptors cover the common sizes from small under-sink units up to 100 GPM, and we set enough of them to know where the gaskets give trouble.     Grease Trap Brands We Install in Raleigh

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Raleigh, NC

    Concrete or polyethylene, a buried gravity interceptor is a two-compartment tank with tees on the inlet and outlet that reach down below the grease layer. Concrete is heavier and holds up under traffic. Poly is easier to set. Plastic tanks in wet soil need hold-down straps or a concrete collar so they do not float when pumped.
