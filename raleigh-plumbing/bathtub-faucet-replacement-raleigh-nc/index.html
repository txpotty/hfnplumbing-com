    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Raleigh

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler.     Upgrading Tub Faucets in Raleigh, NC

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. Old cross handles can become a modern lever trim, and on a universal valve the wall never opens. We can match your sink finish. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Raleigh

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting.     Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Raleigh, NC

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Raleigh

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.
