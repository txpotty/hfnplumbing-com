    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Rocky Mount, NC

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Spot repairs will not keep up.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Rocky Mount

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Rocky Mount, NC

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Rocky Mount

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. Our phone is answered after hours. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. The real repair may be scheduled for the next morning. Until then, we tell you which fixtures are safe to use.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Rocky Mount

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Rocky Mount often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Rocky Mount

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Rocky Mount, NC

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.
