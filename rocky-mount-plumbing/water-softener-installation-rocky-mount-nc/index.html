    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Rocky Mount, NC

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HFN Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Rocky Mount

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater.
