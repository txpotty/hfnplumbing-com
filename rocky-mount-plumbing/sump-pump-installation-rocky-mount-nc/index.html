    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Rocky Mount, NC

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Rocky Mount, NC

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Rocky Mount, NC

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Rocky Mount, NC

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Rocky Mount

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Rocky Mount, NC

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Rocky Mount, NC

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Parts are easy to find. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.
