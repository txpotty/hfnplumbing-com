    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Cary

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it.     Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Cary, NC

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Cary, NC home.
