    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Cary

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Crawlspace foundations are very common here, which makes many plumbing repairs easier to reach than they would be under a slab. We isolate the line first.     Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Cary, NC

    A brown ring on the ceiling under a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. We figure out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe without opening the whole ceiling.

    Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Cary, NC

    One running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons daily. Worn faucet cartridges, softener valves and water heater connectors add more. We dye test tanks, check flappers and fill valves, and inspect supply lines and water heater fittings, and most of these repairs take under an hour with parts from the truck. They are inexpensive repairs.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Cary, NC

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, and on plastic pipe we inject tracer gas and sniff for it at the surface.

    Slab Leak Detection in Cary

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. Look for warm tile first. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then listen through the slab with a ground microphone and confirm with a thermal camera on hot water lines.

    Slab Leak Signs in Cary, NC

We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests, then trace and listen through the slab, and once it is located we go over spot repair, reroute and repipe options with prices for each.
