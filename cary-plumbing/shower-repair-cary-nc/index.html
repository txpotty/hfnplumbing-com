    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Cary, NC

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Cary, NC

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open.     Shower Diverter Repair in Cary, NC

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. It is a short job.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Cary, NC

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Cary

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Cary, NC

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Cary, NC

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. We cut it out with an auger. Heavy buildup further down gets cleared with a water jetter instead of harsh chemicals.
