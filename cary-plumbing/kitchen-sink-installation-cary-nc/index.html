    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Cary, NC

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out needs replacing, not patching. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, so a same-day swap is often possible, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. We swap the old stops as well.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Cary, NC

    The rough-in decides where the drain and valves end up long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Venting happens at this stage. We work from your sink and faucet specs to avoid moving pipe later.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Cary

    Drop-in sinks are common in North Carolina homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Next we connect and test the faucet and drain.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Cary

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Cary, NC

    Undermount and farmhouse installs cost more than a drop-in swap. Each extra, like a new disposal, a moved drain, a lowered wall stub or replacing galvanized stubs, adds labor and shows up as its own line on the quote. The countertop is priced separately.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Cary, NC

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Cary

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Cary

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.
