    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Cary, NC

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. High amp draw points to a weak motor. Low flow with normal amp draw points to a plugged intake or a falling water level, which is a different repair. Testing first saves a pull.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Cary, NC

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Hurricanes and tropical storms can bring heavy rain and flooding to North Carolina, especially in coastal and low-lying areas. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. An arrestor costs little.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Cary, NC

Licensed technicians test these parts safely with the power locked out, replace what failed and tighten every connection in the box. Wiring meets North Carolina code before we restart the pump.
