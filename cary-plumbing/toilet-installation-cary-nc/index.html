    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Cary, NC

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Cary

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Cary

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Cary, NC

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Cary

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. We keep common two-piece toilets on the truck. In many cases we can pull the broken one and set a replacement in the same visit, with a 12 inch rough-in model in round or elongated. Close the stop until then.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Cary, NC

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Cary

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Cary, NC

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. A flushometer needs a 1 inch supply and steady pressure. We confirm the building line can deliver the valve's rated flow, often 20 to 25 gallons a minute, before any fixture goes up. Fixture counts follow code.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Cary

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A grab bar and a taller bowl help a lot. While the wall is open, we add 2 by 6 blocking between the studs at grab bar height, so the bar can later be screwed into solid wood instead of drywall anchors that pull loose. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.
