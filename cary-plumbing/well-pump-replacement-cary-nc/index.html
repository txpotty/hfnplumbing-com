    What We Fix on Well Systems in Cary, NC

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Cary

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Replacement control boxes are easy to source. We match the motor and the wire gauge to the depth of your well.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Cary

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Where the pump hangs matters.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Cary, NC

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. We pick by the well, not the logo. Parts for Goulds and Franklin are easy to get, which matters when a control box dies on a weekend.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Cary

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. Both use a small 2 to 20 gallon tank instead of a big one, since the drive keeps the pump running at a matched speed. The soft start is easier on the motor. Expect to pay more up front.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Cary

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Sand in the impellers and lightning surges are the usual culprits. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. That tells us whether the problem is down the hole or up top.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Cary, NC

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. A correctly sized pump, or a variable-speed drive, can cut the run time. Not always worth it. We will show you the payback.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Cary, NC

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A DC pump and a controller run off a few panels during daylight. You store water, not electricity. Panel count comes from how many gallons you need a day.

    No-Water Calls in Cary

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. Usually the faucet spits air, then quits, after the motor has been limping along for weeks. Before we pull the pump, we test the switch and control box.
