    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Charlotte, NC

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Charlotte

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Charlotte, NC

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Charlotte

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Charlotte, NC

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.
