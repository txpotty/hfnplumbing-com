    Toilet Flush Repair in Charlotte, NC

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Charlotte, NC

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some.     Commercial Toilet Repair in Charlotte

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Charlotte, NC

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. Hardened rubber stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. When one tank seal has failed, we replace the full set so the others do not follow next month.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Charlotte
