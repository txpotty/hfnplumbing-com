    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Charlotte

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Charlotte, NC

    Think of a manifold as a breaker panel for water. A central header feeds each fixture on its own line with its own valve, which lets you shut off one toilet and leave the kitchen running. It uses more pipe. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Charlotte

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. No crimp tool to calibrate. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Charlotte, NC

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines. An addition gets sized on its own. A new bath at the far end of the house may need a three-quarter inch feed to keep pressure up.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Charlotte

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings.
