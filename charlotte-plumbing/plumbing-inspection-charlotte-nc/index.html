    Detailed Plumbing Inspections in Charlotte, NC

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Charlotte, NC

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Charlotte

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Charlotte, NC

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Charlotte

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Charlotte, NC

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. Plan on an hour or so. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Charlotte

    Well owners around Charlotte benefit from periodic water testing and, when needed, neutralizers or filters that preserve pipes and appliances. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Charlotte

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. We check every visible pipe for corrosion and leaks, run each fixture for flow and drainage and test the water heater, the main shutoff and the angle stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Charlotte, NC

    High humidity can rust exposed metal pipe and fittings under homes, so crawlspace plumbing deserves a regular look. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. We look at pipe insulation. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.
