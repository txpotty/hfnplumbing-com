    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom.     Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Charlotte, NC

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Charlotte

    Most gas water heater replacements in Charlotte, NC cost $1,200 to $3,500. Tank size and brand set most of it, and gas line or venting upgrades can push it toward the top of that range. The quote is in writing.

    A permit is required in most of Charlotte and North Carolina for this work. The inspection looks at gas, venting, the relief line and strapping where required. We file it for you.

    Annual flushing and an anode check every few years are the main things. Sediment is what kills most gas tanks, because it sits right over the burner and overheats the steel bottom until it cracks. Have it looked at now and then.

    Ten to twelve years is typical with basic maintenance. Hard water speeds up sediment buildup on the tank bottom, and heavy daily use wears the anode faster, so some tanks quit at eight. Flushing it each year helps.

    Bradford White is our most common tank, with Rheem and Noritz also on the list. Your budget and daily hot water use decide which one we recommend. Parts are easy to get.

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising up a metal flue on its own. Power vent models use a fan to push exhaust out through plastic pipe, which lets them vent sideways through a wall and often burn a bit more efficiently. A power vent needs electricity.
