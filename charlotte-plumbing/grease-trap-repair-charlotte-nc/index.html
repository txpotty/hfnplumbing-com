    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Charlotte, NC

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. On the inlet side, the tee directs incoming water down under the floating grease. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.
