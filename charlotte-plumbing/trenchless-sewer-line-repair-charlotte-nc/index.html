    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Charlotte, NC

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Dense red clay is common in central parts of North Carolina, and it holds water and shifts with the seasons, stressing buried pipes. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under load.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Charlotte

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both systems are made to meet the ASTM standards written for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Charlotte

    We record the full camera run on every job, from the cleanout out to the city main or the septic tank. You get a copy of the video file and a written summary. On the footage you can see what we see, roots at a joint at 22 feet and an offset at 38 feet where the pipe dropped. Those footage marks tell us where to line and where a point repair will do.

    Pipe Bursting in Charlotte, NC

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. The new HDPE pipe is butt-fused into one length first. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. A 60-foot pull often takes under an hour.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Replacement in Charlotte

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Charlotte, NC

    Restaurants feel a sewer failure fast, because grease and a dish machine put heavy load on a 4- or 6-inch building drain all day. Lining can be scheduled overnight or on a day you are closed. A trench through a parking lot means lost spaces and a blocked entrance for a week or more. Liners skip that.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Charlotte, NC

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. On a short, shallow run, a traditional dig can come in lower. Once you add back concrete and sod, the gap usually closes, and we price both options side by side so you can see the difference.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in Charlotte

    Bursting can upsize the line. A 4-inch clay lateral can be replaced with 6-inch HDPE, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. The pit at each end is small. No trench in between.
