    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Charlotte

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job.     Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Charlotte

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Charlotte yard.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Charlotte, NC

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Charlotte, NC soil.
