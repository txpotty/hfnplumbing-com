    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    Commercial Water Softener Repair in Charlotte, NC

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Charlotte

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Charlotte, NC

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Charlotte

    Scale on faucets means trouble. HFN Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, looks at each part of the system and gives you a repair plan for your Charlotte home. We test hardness before and after the repair, so you see the grains per gallon fall.
