    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Charlotte

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. Each element gets an ohm reading. A shorted element will trip the breaker, while an open one just sits there cold, and the readings tell us which one is which. Common elements and thermostats are stocked.

    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Charlotte

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. We test each one, clean the burner and pilot assembly and check draft at the vent. A pilot that lights and then goes out a few seconds later usually points to a weak thermocouple or a clogged flame arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.

    Pilot Light Repair in Charlotte

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Sometimes the cause is a clogged flame arrestor screen starving the burner of air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. If it reads fine, the gas control valve is next.

    Relief Valve and Fitting Leak Repair in Charlotte, NC

    T&P valves drip when the tank is overheating or the house pressure spikes, so replacing the valve alone may not stop it. First we check the thermostat and the water pressure. On homes with a pressure regulator, a missing expansion tank is often the real problem. Loose fittings get tightened or replaced.

    Electric Tank Repair in Charlotte, NC

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Scale from hard water buries the lower element and burns it out. After testing resistance and voltage, we replace what failed, reset the high limit and watch it heat up. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.
