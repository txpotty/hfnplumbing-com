    Propane Water Heater Installation in Charlotte

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We call the propane company when needed. Every joint is leak tested.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Charlotte

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. The old tank gets drained and removed, then the new one goes in with new connectors, a drip leg and a sloped vent. Then we test it. They are dependable and affordable.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Charlotte

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. We connect a new gas flex line with a sediment trap, set the vent connector with a quarter inch per foot of rise and pipe the relief valve to an approved spot. We strap it if code calls for it. Sizing follows household demand.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Charlotte

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.
