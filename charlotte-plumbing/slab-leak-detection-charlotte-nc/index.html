    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Charlotte

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Charlotte

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Urgent Slab Leak Calls in Charlotte, NC

    A slab leak that is soaking carpet or pushing water up through tile cannot wait until next week. Close the main first. HFN Plumbing sends a tech to Charlotte, NC homes quickly, isolates the failed side and gets the rest of the house back on water while the repair gets planned.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Charlotte, NC

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Charlotte, NC home.
