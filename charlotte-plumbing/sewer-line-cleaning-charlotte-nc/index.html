    What Our Charlotte, NC Sewer Line Crews Handle

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Charlotte

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Cracks let them in. Roots seek out moisture and nutrients through tiny gaps, and we use root cutters and jetting to clear them from the line, then explain options to keep them from coming back.

    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Charlotte, NC

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Sewer odors, slow drains in several rooms and water rising in the tub when the toilet flushes are all warning signs.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Charlotte, NC

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. Cables only open a path. High-pressure water strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes them toward the street, and with the right settings it is safe for PVC and older clay alike.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Charlotte

    Repeat problems call for a camera. It shows the cause. We look inside the line to see what is causing the trouble, then choose the cheapest fix that will hold.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Charlotte, NC

    Roots are the top cause of blockages in established neighborhoods. Waste catches on them. After cutting the mass with a root saw, we jet the line to clear root hairs and leave the pipe clean, then show each entry point on camera.

    Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Charlotte, NC

    Sewer backups pick bad times. Guests make it worse. Our emergency team handles urgent blockages and overflows with main line equipment and cleanup gear, getting the line open and limiting damage as quickly as possible.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Charlotte

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. It narrows the line over time. Our tech runs a cable from the cleanout with a blade sized for the line, then jets the walls if needed, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.
