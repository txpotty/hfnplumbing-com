    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Durham, NC Home

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Durham

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most North Carolina homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We measure the closet before ordering. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Durham

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Durham, NC

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. Choosing is simple.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Durham

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. The venting changes too. We check meter capacity and pipe length before quoting so there are no surprises halfway through the job.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Durham, NC

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. Those burners need more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Durham, NC

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Durham, NC

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. We replace damaged sections and the draft hood if needed. We check draft with a match or mirror at the hood.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Durham

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. Sizing and gas hookup are part of the job. Exhaust venting gets checked against their specs.
