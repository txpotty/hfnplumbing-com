    How We Set New Sinks in Durham, NC

    Undermount Sink Installation in Durham

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Durham

    A cracked bowl cannot be patched for long. When a bowl cracks or a drain rusts through, we cap the lines first so the house keeps water, then set a new sink. Stock sizes are often same-day. Odd cutouts take longer.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Durham

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard.     Drop-In Sink Installation in Durham, NC

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Durham

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. Swapping a chipped drop-in for a new one in the same cutout usually takes a morning, and moving up to a deeper single bowl may only need a new trap and a short drain extension. New counters add a day. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Durham, NC

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Moving the drain adds the most. We price each piece on the quote.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Durham

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Anchors in drywall will fail. A loose basin cracks the trap.
