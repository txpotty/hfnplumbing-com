    Hydro Jetting in Durham, NC

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Durham

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Durham, NC

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Durham, NC

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Durham

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain means the main is blocked. Stop using water first. Our technician opens the line, then jets it to remove the cause, and a camera pass after jetting shows whether the backup came from buildup alone or from a broken or root-filled section that needs repair.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Durham, NC

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Durham

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Durham

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Durham, NC

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.
