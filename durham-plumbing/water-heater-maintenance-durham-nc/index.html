    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Durham

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. The tank drains until the water is clean. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Durham, NC

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. Carbon monoxide is the concern. We also test the thermocouple.

    Water Heater Maintenance Plans in Durham, NC

    A plan takes the remembering off your plate. Members get a yearly or twice-yearly visit on the calendar, a reminder before it is due, priority scheduling if the heater acts up and discounted rates on any repair we find. Some homes need two visits. Service records stay with us.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Durham, NC

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Durham

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. We swap worn ones. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Durham

    Wrong temperature means a part is off. Electric units get thermostat and element tests, and gas units get burner, pilot and control checks. High humidity can rust exposed metal pipe and fittings under homes, so crawlspace plumbing deserves a regular look. Faulty parts waste energy. They also cut your hot water short.
