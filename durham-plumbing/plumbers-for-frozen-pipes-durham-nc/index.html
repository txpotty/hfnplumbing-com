    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbers for Frozen Pipes in Durham

    Thawing alone usually runs a couple hundred dollars. After-hours calls cost more. The price depends on how hard the frozen section is to reach, how long it takes to thaw, and whether the line needs repair once it is flowing again, which we quote before starting that part.

    You can open the faucet and gently warm an exposed pipe with a hair dryer. Open flame is never safe here. If the frozen section is inside a wall, a ceiling or under the floor, call a plumber instead, because you cannot see whether it has already split, and thawing it can release water into the structure.

    Pipe location matters more than brand. Copper and galvanized split first. Metal pipe in cold spots splits most, and PEX often survives except at the fittings.

    A faucet that trickles or runs dry after a cold night is the most common sign. Frost on exposed pipe is another. A toilet that will not refill or a bulging pipe also points to ice.

    A frost-free hose bib has a long stem that shuts off water inside the warm part of the wall. The outside part drains when you close it. It only works if the hose is disconnected, because a connected hose traps water in the stem where it can freeze and split the valve inside the wall, which you may not notice until spring.

    Many policies cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, though coverage varies. Read your policy. Some policies exclude damage if the house was left unheated or the water was not shut off while you were away, and we can give you an invoice, photos and a description of the failure to support your claim.

    A new freeze is not a warranty issue, but it is a warning sign. The line is still too cold. We quote a permanent fix, since patching the same run every winter costs more.

    No pipe is freeze-proof. PEX tolerates freezing better than most. It stretches instead of splitting, but its fittings can still fail, so placement and insulation matter most.
