    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Durham, NC

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Well owners around Durham benefit from periodic water testing and, when needed, neutralizers or filters that protect pipes and appliances. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Durham, NC

    Besides repairs, HFN Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, like a restaurant adding a dish line or a second restroom. Bigger meters need bigger pipe. We size it to the fixture load.     Water Pressure Restoration in Durham

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the main shutoff and the curb valve for anything half closed, and look at the pipe itself. Galvanized pipe can lose half its opening to rust scale. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    After-Hours Water Line Breaks in Durham, NC

    Water lines tend to break on a Saturday night. Ours is a 24 hour line. HFN Plumbing sends a plumber who can shut off the curb stop, pump the hole and make a permanent repair on the spot, so you are not stuck with a temporary clamp until Monday. If the break is on the utility side of the meter, we tell you right away and help you get the provider out. It is more common than most people expect.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Durham

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Durham

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Older homes around Durham may have galvanized steel supply lines or cast iron drains that are reaching the end of their life. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. We price each one for you.

    Main Water Line Repair in Durham

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Durham

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.
