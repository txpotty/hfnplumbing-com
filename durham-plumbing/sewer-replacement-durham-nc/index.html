    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Durham, NC

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Durham

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. One offset joint near the house is a repair. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet mean the rest of the pipe is close behind, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Durham

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. There is often a grease trap or interceptor to work around. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and work with the utility on the main connection. Weekend starts are common.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Durham, NC

    Diagnosis comes before price. The camera shows what is wrong, the locator shows where. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, and a clean pipe gives a much more honest picture of its condition. Guesswork costs money.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Durham, NC

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. Lining patches the inside of the old one. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, because it leaves you with brand new pipe rather than a liner inside an old one, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Durham

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, but not forever. The inside rusts and scales until the bottom of the pipe thins out and cracks. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Durham

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Durham, NC

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Durham

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. Modern PVC does not rust, flatten or let roots in at glued joints. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, and we match the grade to the depth and load over the pipe. Glued joints stay sealed.

    Local Sewer Replacement Near Me in Durham, NC

    A local crew can come back for the inspection. That matters with sewer work. The trench usually has to stay open until the inspector signs off, so we plan our schedule in Durham around the inspection window and keep the hole covered, fenced and marked overnight until it passes.

    Our crews work on sewer lines across Durham every week. Whole blocks were often built with the same pipe. If the house next door had clay pipe full of roots, yours probably does too, which gives us a good guess before we scope. Patterns help.
