    Emergency Sink Drain Service in Durham

    A trap that falls apart sends a sink of water into the cabinet. Shut off the faucet, put a bucket under it, and avoid running water elsewhere if another drain is backing up. We send a plumber with trap kits. Most get fixed for good on the first visit.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Durham

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The outlet on the disposal has to sit above the trap inlet, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or an air gap, and the trap has to line up without the slip joints pulling sideways. We set them all at once. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Durham

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. We replace the strainer and trap together. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Durham, NC

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub. A cracked trap arm or a stub rusted through behind the escutcheon will drip down the inside of the cabinet back panel, where you only notice it when the wood swells or the cabinet smells musty. The bad section gets cut back. Then new PVC gets tied in.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Durham, NC

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Patching one piece rarely works. The new drain matches your faucet finish.
