    Jetting Versus Snaking Costs in Durham, NC

    Jetting costs more per visit than a cable. But if you are paying for the same clog three or four times a year, a single jetting visit that holds for a year or more usually comes out cheaper by the second season. One-off hair clogs just need a cable. You see both prices before we start.

    Floor Drain and Utility Sink Jetting in Durham, NC

    Garage floor drains get forgotten. Mud, paint rinse, sawdust and sand from utility and mop sinks settle in the trap and the line beyond it until a heavy wash day sends water across the floor. We jet both. A dry trap lets sewer gas in, so we check the primer too.

    Commercial Drain Jetting in Durham, NC

    Commercial drains carry heavier loads. A busy restaurant puts more grease down its lines in a week than a house does in a year. Bigger jetters handle this. Most commercial work gets done before opening or after close so nobody loses a service.

    Jetting Equipment We Use in Durham, NC
