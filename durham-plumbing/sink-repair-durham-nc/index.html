    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Durham

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Durham, NC

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. Close the angle stops while you wait. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Durham, NC

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full.     Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Durham, NC

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Durham, NC

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Durham

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute.     Leaking P-Trap Fix in Durham
