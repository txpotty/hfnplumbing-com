    Water Softener Maintenance Work We Do in Durham, NC

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Durham

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. Raw water gets tested from an unsoftened hose bib or with the valve on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.

    Resin Rebedding in Durham

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Durham, NC

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. We rebuild or replace the bypass and check the drain line for kinks. Code requires an air gap at the drain.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Durham, NC

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 when they show wear.

    Emergency Softener Repairs in Durham, NC

    A softener stuck in backwash can run water to the drain all night, and a cracked brine tank can empty across the floor. Both are same-day problems. First, put the valve in bypass so the house still has water, then call (980) 364-4317 and we will get a plumber out.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Durham

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a piston and seal kit swap, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Durham, NC

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Durham, NC

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.
