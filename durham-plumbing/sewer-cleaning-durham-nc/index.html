    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Durham, NC

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Well owners around Durham benefit from periodic water testing and, when needed, neutralizers or filters that protect pipes and appliances.     Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Durham, NC

    Emergency Sewer Cleaning in Durham

    Sewage on the floor cannot wait. We take emergency calls at night. The emergency truck carries a main line machine and cleanup gear, so we can open the line and contain the water on the same visit.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Durham

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Durham

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. A sewer joint is a steady source. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out, then follow with a jetter if fine root hairs are left behind, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    Root Masses in Durham Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Durham, NC

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.
