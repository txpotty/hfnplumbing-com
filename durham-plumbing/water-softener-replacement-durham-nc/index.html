    Picking the Right Softener for Durham, NC Water

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Durham

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. We work around your business hours.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Durham

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Use pellet salt.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Durham

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Durham

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. You will buy less salt. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Durham

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Durham, NC

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Durham, NC

    Swapping a salt-based softener in North Carolina homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. The drain line gets an air gap.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Durham, NC

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    A local plumber can also check how the softener ties into your water heater and main line. We spot loop leaks and drain problems that cut performance, like a drain line with no air gap or a bypass valve that was left half open. Quotes are straightforward.
