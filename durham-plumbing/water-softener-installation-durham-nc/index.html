    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Durham

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Durham

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Durham, NC

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Durham

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Durham

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Durham

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Durham, NC

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HFN Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.
