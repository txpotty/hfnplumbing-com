    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Concord, NC

    Upfront, a tankless install in Concord, NC costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows.     Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Concord

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. With a shower at about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen tap a bit under, two showers and a sink together need around 5 or 6 gallons per minute at the needed rise. That narrows the model list fast.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Concord, NC

    Businesses in Concord, NC with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank.     Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Concord, NC

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and replacement parts are widely stocked.     Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Concord

    Commercial hot water loads in Concord run longer and harder than a house.     Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Concord, NC

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Concord, NC homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current.     Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Concord

    Gas tankless units in Concord are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. Gas is the catch. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.
