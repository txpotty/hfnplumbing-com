    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Concord

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. Recirculating Rinnai models also have a pump setting worth checking, since a wrong schedule wastes gas every hour the pump runs for no reason.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Concord, NC

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. Gas pressure at the inlet gets read with a manometer at full fire and checked against the rating plate. The flue gets a look for obstructions. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Concord

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Noritz Descaling in Concord, NC

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Concord, NC

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Concord, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. We follow the manufacturer's steps, not a generic checklist. That matters for warranty claims down the road.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Concord, NC

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. Descaling costs far less.     Service Valve Kit Installs in Concord, NC

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. Future flushes then take minutes to set up.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Concord

    Municipal water in North Carolina is generally soft, but many private wells produce acidic water that can corrode copper pipe over time. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. Flow comes back. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.
