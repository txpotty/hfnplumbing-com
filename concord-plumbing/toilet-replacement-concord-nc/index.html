    Common Toilet Replacements in Concord, NC

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Concord

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. For a cracked flange, a stainless repair ring is often enough, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Concord

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. It needs to be replaced. The cracked unit comes out, and we look at the floor under it before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Concord

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Concord, NC

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. A WaterSense model uses 1.28 gallons or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Some water providers offer a rebate, so ask before you buy.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Concord

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. You can pick battery or hardwired sensors. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Concord, NC

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts.
