    How We Set New Sinks in Concord, NC

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Concord

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Concord, NC

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Concord, NC

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Concord, NC

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Concord

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. A same-size drop-in swap usually takes a morning, and a deeper bowl may only need a new trap. New counters add a day. We tell you which one you are looking at.
