    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Concord, NC

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well.     Recurring Clog Repair in Concord

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Find it and fix it.     Bathroom Drain Repair in Concord

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Hard water makes it worse. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, plus tub and shower drain leaks that stain the room underneath. Figure on about an hour for a typical fix.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Concord, NC

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Sewer Drain Line Repair in Concord

    Every drain in the house empties into one building drain. One blockage, every fixture. Cracks, offsets and collapses in that line cause whole-house backups, and we repair them with trenchless methods when the pipe qualifies or a small targeted dig when it does not. The camera tells us which.

    Drain Vent Repair in Concord, NC

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. The drain cannot breathe. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, which is why we inspect the roof opening and the vent piping inside the walls. Clearing it is often quick.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Concord

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Some come back fast.
