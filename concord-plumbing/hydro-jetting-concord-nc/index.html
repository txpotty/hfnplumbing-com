    Kitchen Line Grease Jetting in Concord, NC

    Grease clings and hardens. Flat runs are the worst. Jetting melts and scrubs that buildup away instead of poking a hole through it, using smaller hose and nozzles sized for 1-1/2 and 2 inch kitchen lines, so your sink and dishwasher drain properly for much longer.

    Commercial Hydro Jetting in Concord, NC

    Commercial pipe sees heavy loads. Kitchens add grease every shift. Bigger jetters with more flow handle commercial lines, which keeps the kitchen open and the health inspector satisfied.

    Large-Line Commercial Jetting in Concord, NC

    Big buildings need trailer jetters. More flow, longer reach. Restaurants, food processors, hotels and apartment complexes create heavy grease and solids loads, and we clean interceptor outlets, floor drains and main laterals on a schedule that fits your operation, with written records for inspections.

    Hydro Jetting Before Pipe Lining in Concord, NC

    Liners need clean pipe. Buildup keeps them from sealing. The old pipe gets jetted and inspected, and any remaining obstructions are cut out so the liner can bond and seal, since skipping prep can lead to wrinkles or poor bonding.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydro Jetting in Concord

    Mature trees near old sewers cause trouble. They enter at joints and cracks. Jetting cuts and washes out roots far more completely than a snake, which buys more time before regrowth.

    Root Cutting With Hydro Jetting in Concord

    Specialty nozzles slice fine roots. Cables miss those. Roots from yard trees and shrubs often enter at clay pipe joints or cracks, and jetting clears them out and gives a clean view of each entry point on camera afterward, which helps you weigh lining against a spot repair.

    Main Sewer Line Hydro Jetting in Concord

    Every drain in the house empties into the main. A clog there stops them all. Roots from nearby trees are the usual cause, and a jetter cuts them out and flushes the line clean to the street.
