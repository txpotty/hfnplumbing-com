    Repiping Work We Handle in Concord, NC

    Drain Line Repiping in Concord

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. A camera shows the condition. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Concord

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Concord, NC

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. One slab leak with access and repair often costs $1,000 to $4,000. Do the math. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Concord, NC

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Concord

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. Rerouting one branch overhead and leaving the bad pipe in the slab is often cheaper than both a repipe and a jackhammer repair. It suits a first-time leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Concord

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Coastal areas have sandy soil and high water tables, which affect how sewer lines and septic systems function. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Concord, NC

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    Copper Repiping in Concord, NC

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Copper takes a bit longer. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.
