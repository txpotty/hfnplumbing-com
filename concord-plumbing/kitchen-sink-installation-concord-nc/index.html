    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Concord, NC: The Details

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Concord

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Concord, NC

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Concord

    Cheap stainless bowls are noisy when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before we set it. It is a small thing. Once the bowl is mounted, the space underneath is hard to reach, so this is a job worth doing while the sink is still on the floor.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Concord, NC

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. We plan the drain early. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Concord, NC

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Concord

    A cracked bowl or a rusted-through strainer can soak a cabinet in an afternoon. First, close both angle stops and stop using the sink. We carry common sizes. For a standard 33 by 22 inch double bowl drop-in, we can often pull the old sink and set a new one the same day.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Concord, NC

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.
