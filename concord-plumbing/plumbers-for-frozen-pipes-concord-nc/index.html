    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Concord

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Those get heat cable. We replace old constant-wattage tape with listed self-regulating cable.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Concord, NC

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. Exposed runs get heat cable. We install self-regulating cable that adjusts its output to the pipe temperature, plugged into a GFCI outlet, and cover it with insulation so it uses less power and keeps the pipe above freezing on the coldest nights.
