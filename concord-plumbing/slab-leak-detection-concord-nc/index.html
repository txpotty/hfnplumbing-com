    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Concord, NC

    Older homes in Concord, NC often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both corrode with age. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. We know those failure points. That knowledge points the search at the likeliest spots first.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Concord

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Concord, NC home.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Concord, NC

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, ending the cycle of floor openings and surprise water bills. A full repipe takes about two to four days.

    Urgent Slab Leak Calls in Concord, NC

    A slab leak that is soaking carpet or pushing water up through tile cannot wait until next week. Shut off the main. HFN Plumbing sends a tech to Concord, NC homes quickly, isolates the failed side and gets the rest of the house back on water while the repair gets planned.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Concord

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. Tile and hardwood floors make this the favorite.

    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Concord, NC

    Large open floors can hide a leak. A thermal camera scans an entire living area in a few minutes, showing temperature differences of a degree or less across tile or concrete, which lets us rule out large areas before acoustic work starts. That saves real time in bigger houses across Concord, NC.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Concord, NC

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Concord

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.
