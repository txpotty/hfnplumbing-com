    Fixing Showers in Concord, NC

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Concord

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Concord, NC

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Concord, NC

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Concord, NC

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Concord

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. We fix the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Concord, NC

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Then call us. HFN Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Concord, NC

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Concord

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Close the main, set a bucket under the drip and stay clear of any drywall that is bulging, because wet ceilings can drop. Then give us a call. We find the source, stop it and dry out what we can that day.
