    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Concord

    These disposals run only with the stopper lid seated, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Concord

    On a batch-feed unit, the lid is the switch. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. It is the safety feature. Families with kids and anyone nervous about a hand near the hopper tend to choose these in Concord homes.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Concord, NC

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Concord

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.

    Disposal Rough-In for New Construction in Concord

    At rough-in we set the drain height and the outlet location before the cabinets go in. For a kitchen with a disposal, we usually set the drain stub lower than a plain sink would need, so the trap can sit below the disposal outlet with room for proper fall. A switched outlet goes in at this stage. Its location gets marked to stay clear of the trap.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Concord, NC

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. Bigger units are heavier. We support them while mounting.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Concord

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. We install the mount, hang the disposal and connect the discharge tube to a new trap, lowering the drain at the wall if the old stub sits too high for the disposal outlet. An electrician adds power if needed.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Concord

    With a typical 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Concord homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Cramped cabinets take some planning. A compact unit can solve it.
