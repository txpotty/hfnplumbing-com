    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Concord

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Concord, NC

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Concord, NC

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Flat runs collect more debris. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Concord

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. We set up outside at the cleanout whenever we can, so the jetter hose never has to come through your house or across your floors. Some kitchen lines get jetted from under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Concord

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Stop using water first. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Concord

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Main Line Jetting in Concord, NC

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Concord

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Hydrojet Plumbing Near Me in Concord, NC

    Working locally helps us plan each job before we arrive. Old clay calls for a root head. Restaurant lines get grease nozzles and short passes, and cast iron in older homes gets lower pressure and a scale nozzle, so the right setup is ready instead of improvised in your driveway.

    A local jetting crew knows what pipe to expect on each street. Older blocks usually mean clay. Newer neighborhoods have PVC that takes full pressure, while older ones need gentler settings, and knowing that before we arrive means the right nozzles are already on the truck.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Concord

    Residential hydrojetting in Concord typically costs $400 to $900, including a camera inspection before and after. A snake is cheaper per visit. Because jetting removes the buildup instead of punching through it, many homeowners end up spending less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking for the same line.

    It depends on the line. A cart unit handles most houses. Sewer mains and commercial lines get a trailer jetter with more flow, since flow is what carries loosened debris out of a 4-inch or 6-inch pipe, while pressure is what breaks the buildup loose.

    We use cart and trailer jetters from manufacturers like Spartan and RIDGID. Each job gets its own nozzle. We carry penetrating nozzles for packed lines, spinning nozzles for grease and scale, and root-cutting heads, plus a camera with a locator so we can mark any defect on the surface.

    Call us within the guarantee period and we come back. Usually there is a reason. A clog that returns soon after a thorough jetting usually means a defect in the pipe, like a belly, an offset joint or a crack letting roots in, and the camera footage from the first visit tells us where to look.

    Clogs that return within weeks of snaking are the clearest sign. Slow drains in several rooms are another. Gurgling, sewer odor and kitchen backups suggest buildup a cable will not remove.

    It is safe for most sound PVC, ABS, cast iron and clay. Pressure varies by pipe type. Thin-wall pipe, old Orangeburg fiber pipe and any line with cracks or separated joints get lower pressure or no jetting at all, which is why the camera inspection happens before we ever turn the jetter on.

    Most home jetting jobs take one to three hours. That includes the camera work. Heavy commercial lines can take most of a day since we work in passes.

    Jetting fits when the same line clogs again and again. The camera shows the buildup. Buildup means jetting, and broken pipe means repair.
