    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Concord

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. PVC and HDPE do none of that. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Concord, NC

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem. With only one bad section, we excavate a single small pit and splice in new PVC using shielded couplings. That costs much less. You see the footage that supports the call.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Concord, NC

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. Lining is the other trenchless route. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Concord, NC

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. We fuse the HDPE into one continuous length on the lawn. Most bursts finish in a day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.
