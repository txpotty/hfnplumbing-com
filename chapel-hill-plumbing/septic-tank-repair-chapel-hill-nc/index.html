    Septic Tee Repair in Chapel Hill

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Chapel Hill

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. An alarm going off means the water rose past the high-level float. We check the breaker and meter the pump draw before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in Chapel Hill

    Gurgling drains right after a pump-out point to the line between the house and the tank, not the tank itself. That section sags or breaks from roots and settling soil. A camera from the house cleanout finds the bad joint, and we mark it in the yard. Then we dig one hole, not a trench. A second cleanout near the tank goes in while it is open.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Chapel Hill, NC

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age.     Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Chapel Hill

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. Each maintenance call includes a sludge and scum reading, and pumping is due once solids fill roughly a third of the tank. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Chapel Hill, NC

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.
