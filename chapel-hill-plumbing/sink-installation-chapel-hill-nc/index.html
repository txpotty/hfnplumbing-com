    Drop-In Sink Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Chapel Hill

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Chapel Hill

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.
