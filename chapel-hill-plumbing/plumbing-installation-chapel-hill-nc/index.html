    What We Install in Chapel Hill, NC

    PEX Repiping in Chapel Hill

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. It also handles a freeze better. Hard freezes are uncommon along the coast but happen most winters in the mountains, where pipes in crawlspaces and outside walls need protection. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    Electric tankless heaters pull serious amperage, often 120 amps or more for a whole-house unit. Before we quote one, we check that your panel and service can carry that load, and we bring in a licensed electrician when they cannot. The plumbing side is simpler. Flow and temperature get dialed in so a running dishwasher does not turn the shower cold.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Chapel Hill

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. Each one is tested right after installation by a certified backflow tester, and the report goes to the water purveyor. That starts the annual clock. Retesting is usually yearly, and we keep a reminder list so you do not miss the date.

    Water Heater Installation in Chapel Hill

    Tank or tankless, gas or electric, the first question is how many people shower in the morning. We size the heater to that peak demand and your fuel source, then check the venting and gas supply before quoting. Gas heaters need the closest look. A draft hood that spills or a flue with the wrong pitch can push carbon monoxide back into the house. We haul the old tank away the same day.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. Pump and basin are sized to the fixtures, the basin is vented to code, and the discharge gets a check valve and a shutoff so the pump can come out for service. Brands we trust here are Liberty and Zoeller.

    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Chapel Hill, NC

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service. We add up the BTU demand of everything on the line, measure the run, and size each branch so the furthest appliance still gets full pressure. Every new line is pressure tested. Bigger loads sometimes need a meter upgrade, and we set that up with the gas utility.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Gas models usually need a bigger gas line and new stainless or PVC venting. Electric models need amperage. We size by gallons per minute and the temperature rise from your incoming water. High humidity can rust exposed metal pipe and fittings under homes, so crawlspace plumbing deserves a regular look. Isolation valves go on every unit for descaling.

    Water Softener Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    Well owners around Chapel Hill benefit from periodic water testing and, when needed, neutralizers or filters that preserve pipes and appliances. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. A hardness test at the kitchen tap, in grains per gallon, tells us whether a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner fits your usage. The bypass valve is not optional. It lets you shut the unit off for service without losing water to the house.
