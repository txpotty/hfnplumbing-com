    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Chapel Hill

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Chapel Hill

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Chapel Hill, NC

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Chapel Hill, NC

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Then we check the fan and the combustion air path. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.
