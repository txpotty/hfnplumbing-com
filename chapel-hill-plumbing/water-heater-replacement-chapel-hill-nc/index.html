    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Chapel Hill

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. When a tank starts leaking at the base, making loud popping sounds or giving rusty hot water, we replace it like-for-like or resize it, and the whole job usually takes two to four hours from shutoff to first hot shower. You shower tonight.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Chapel Hill

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Chapel Hill, NC

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Leaks only get worse.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Chapel Hill

    Businesses in Chapel Hill put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons and offices all draw differently, so we size by peak gallons per hour and required temperature, which may mean a larger tank, a booster or a tankless rack. We schedule around your hours.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Chapel Hill

    Electric water heaters in Chapel Hill usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Chapel Hill, NC

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs. Bradford White is trade-only and durable, Rheem offers range and parts, and AO Smith brings plenty of sizes, so the pick comes down to fit, warranty and stock. All three hold up.

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. We will say when repair works.
