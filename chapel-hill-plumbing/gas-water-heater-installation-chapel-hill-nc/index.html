    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Chapel Hill

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Chapel Hill

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. We inspect the existing vent. We replace bad sections.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Installation in Chapel Hill

    Restaurants and laundromats can drain a residential tank in minutes. For businesses we install high-input heaters with bigger gas lines and dedicated vents, sometimes in banks of two or three. Some uses need 140 degrees. Installs can happen after close.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Chapel Hill

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. A conversion often means a larger gas line, new venting, a condensate drain and service valves. Plan on a full day. We do all of it to code.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.
