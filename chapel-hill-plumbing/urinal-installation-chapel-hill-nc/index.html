    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go. A 2 inch drain, a vent tie-in and a supply sized to the valve go in, with blocking or a compact carrier. Many use 0.125 gallons per flush. That barely touches the water bill.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Chapel Hill, NC

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. We install battery or hardwired units, set the sensing range so the valve does not fire when someone walks past, and turn on a sanitary flush cycle if the restroom sits empty for long stretches. Batteries last a few years. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Chapel Hill, NC

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. Permits are on us. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Chapel Hill

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Chapel Hill

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. We set the carrier so the finished rim lands at 17 inches or lower, keep the clear floor space free of partitions and door swings, and mount the flush handle or sensor where a seated user can reach it. Measurements get checked twice. Inspectors measure too.
