    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater.     Commercial Water Softener Installation in Greenville, NC

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Greenville, NC

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HFN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Greenville

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Greenville

Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.
