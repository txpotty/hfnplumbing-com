    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Greenville, NC

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Redundancy matters here.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Greenville

    Zoeller's sewage line runs from light residential to heavy duty, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. The model comes from the pump curve for your lift, and most basement baths need a four-tenths or half horsepower unit that passes two-inch solids. Bigger lifts need more.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Greenville

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Greenville

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Heavy summer thunderstorms are common too, so working gutters, yard drainage and sump pumps help keep water away from foundations. Battery backup is an option.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Greenville

    Liberty ejector pumps show up on a lot of our installs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Liberty sells packaged systems with basin, lid, pump and check valve already matched, which means less field fitting and a listed assembly for inspection. We still add a shutoff.
