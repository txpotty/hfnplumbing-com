    Gas Line Repair Costs in Greenville, NC

    Simple connector or fitting repairs often cost $150 to $450. Interior section replacements tend to land between $400 and $1,200, and buried line replacements often run $1,000 to $4,000 or more.     Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Greenville

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Greenville

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    CSST Repair in Greenville

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. Bonding gets checked on every system. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Greenville, NC

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Honest Gas Line Repair Pricing in Greenville, NC

    Gas leaks are usually a surprise expense, so the price should not be another surprise. You get a written estimate covering the work, the parts and any permit fees before anything begins. No hidden fees. If spot repair and replacement are both options, we price each so you can weigh cost now against the age of what is left in the ground.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Greenville, NC

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Greenville, NC

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. Utilities get marked first. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.
