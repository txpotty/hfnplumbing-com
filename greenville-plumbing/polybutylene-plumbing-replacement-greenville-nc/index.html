    Why Polybutylene Fails in Greenville

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Municipal water in North Carolina is generally soft, but many private wells produce acidic water that can corrode copper pipe over time. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
