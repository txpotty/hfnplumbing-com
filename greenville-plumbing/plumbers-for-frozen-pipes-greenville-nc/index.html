    Choosing a plumber for frozen pipes depends on their thawing method and what they test afterward. Open flame starts fires. We use electric and infrared heat, then pressure test the line after it thaws, because a pipe can look fine with the water off and still have a hairline split that opens up under full pressure.

    • Safe Thawing, We thaw with controlled, flame-free heat matched to the pipe material.
    • Lasting Repairs, We repair splits with copper or PEX-A, then pressure test the line.

    Citywide Coverage

    Newer homes with attic PEX and older houses with exposed galvanized both get freeze calls. PEX tolerates ice better. PEX can often stretch without splitting, while galvanized and copper usually crack, but PEX fittings and the brass or plastic connections at each end can still fail, so we test every line after thawing.

    Experienced Team

    Experience across older and newer homes lets our team move quickly during cold snaps. Every house freezes differently. Crawlspace homes freeze below, slab homes at walls and attics, and garage heaters at the connections.
