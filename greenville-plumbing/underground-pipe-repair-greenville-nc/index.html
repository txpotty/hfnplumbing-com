    Pipe Bursting Services in Greenville, NC

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Foundation Leak Detection and Repair in Greenville, NC

    A leak under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor. Sometimes you hear water running. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging, then repair it through a small slab cut or reroute the line overhead so the floor stays intact. Both options get a price.

    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Greenville

    Digging a trench across a mature yard is expensive to restore. Trenchless avoids most of it. We open small access pits at each end, then pull new pipe through the old path or line the old pipe in place, so the expense of putting the yard back together shrinks a lot. Faster, too.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Greenville

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. One or two holes, not a trench.
