    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Greenville

    Flow rating is the number that matters on a hydromechanical trap, and it has to match the fastest-draining fixture combination in your kitchen.     Automatic Grease Removal Units in Greenville

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. They cost more up front.
