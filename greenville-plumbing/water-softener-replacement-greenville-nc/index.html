    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Greenville, NC

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again.     Dual Tank Softener Systems in Greenville

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Greenville, NC

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in North Carolina homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Greenville

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.
