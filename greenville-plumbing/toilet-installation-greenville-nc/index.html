    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Greenville, NC

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first.     Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Greenville

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Greenville, NC

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Greenville, NC
