    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Greenville, NC

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. They are common on islands.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Greenville, NC

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in Greenville

    On new builds and remodels in Greenville, we coordinate with the general contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together. Early planning prevents rework.
