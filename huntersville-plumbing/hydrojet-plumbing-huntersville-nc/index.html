    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Huntersville

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Huntersville

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass.     Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Huntersville, NC

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Huntersville, NC
