    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Huntersville, NC

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Huntersville

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.
