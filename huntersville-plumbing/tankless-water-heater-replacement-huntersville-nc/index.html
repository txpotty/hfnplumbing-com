    Picking a Tankless for Your Huntersville, NC Home

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Huntersville, NC

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. Both readings get taken.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Huntersville, NC

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Hard freezes are uncommon along the coast but happen most winters in the mountains, where pipes in crawlspaces and outside walls require protection. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Huntersville

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Huntersville, NC

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Most of the plumbing lines up already.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Huntersville

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Plan on four to six hours.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Huntersville

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Most take under two hours.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Huntersville, NC

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. The old flue gets capped.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Huntersville, NC

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.
