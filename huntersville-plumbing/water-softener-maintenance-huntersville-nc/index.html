    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Huntersville, NC

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Huntersville, NC

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.
