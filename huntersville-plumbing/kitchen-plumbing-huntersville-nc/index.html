    Burst Supply Lines in Huntersville, NC

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put several gallons a minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. Both supply lines get replaced. If one line cracked from age, the other one is about the same age.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in Huntersville, NC

    A hum without grinding tells us the motor has power and the plate will not turn. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. No sound at all points us to the switch, the outlet or the breaker.

    Kitchen Drain Clog Removal in Huntersville, NC

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line inside the wall.
