    Sewer Camera Inspections in Huntersville, NC

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Huntersville, NC

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Homes built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene pipe, which is known for sudden failures. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Huntersville, NC

    Commercial lines take a beating. Restaurants fight grease every day. In offices and shops, paper and odd objects flushed in restrooms are the usual cause of a stopped lateral, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Huntersville

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. A sewer joint is a steady source.     Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.
