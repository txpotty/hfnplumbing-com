    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Apex, NC

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Apex

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in North Carolina houses. That list starts with old supply pipe, worn angle stops, aging water heaters, slow drains and sewer lines with roots or sags. We test each one directly. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Apex, NC

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Some need replacing soon. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Apex

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Water Heater Inspections in Apex

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Apex, NC

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Apex, NC

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Apex, NC

    Hot, humid summers are the main climate stress in North Carolina, bringing condensation on cold pipes and moisture problems in crawlspaces. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. We look at pipe insulation. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.
