    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Apex, NC

    Swapping a salt-based softener in North Carolina homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Apex

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Apex

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. A tank water heater on hard water can lose a noticeable share of its efficiency as scale builds on the bottom, and tankless units scale up even faster. Soft water prevents it.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Apex

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Use pellet salt.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Apex, NC

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.
