    Undermount Sink Installation in Apex

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Apex, NC

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Apex

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Apex, NC

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.
