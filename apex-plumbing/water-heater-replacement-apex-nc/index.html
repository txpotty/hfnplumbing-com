    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Apex, NC homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes.     Electric Water Heater Replacement in Apex

    Electric water heaters in Apex usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap.     Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Apex
