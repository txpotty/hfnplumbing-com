    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Apex, NC

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Apex

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Coastal areas have sandy soil and high water tables, which affect how sewer lines and septic systems perform. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Apex, NC

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Apex, NC

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Apex

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Apex, NC

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Nails, rodents and lightning can all damage CSST, and older yellow-jacket tubing must be bonded to the home's grounding system to lower arc risk. Bonding is a short job. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. Heavy summer thunderstorms are common too, so reliable gutters, yard drainage and sump pumps help keep water away from foundations.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Apex, NC
