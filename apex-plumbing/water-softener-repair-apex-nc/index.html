    Resin Testing and Replacement in Apex

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush.     Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Apex, NC

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold.
