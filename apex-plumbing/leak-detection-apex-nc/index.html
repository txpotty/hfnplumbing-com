    Gas Line Leak Locating in Apex

    You cannot locate a gas leak by smell alone. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe can signal a leak that has run for a long time. Utility coordination is included.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Apex, NC

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Apex

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Pool Leak Detection in Apex

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, use an underwater hydrophone to listen, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain so we can point to the exact fitting or crack.

    Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Apex, NC

    One running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons daily. Worn faucet cartridges, softener valves and water heater connectors add more. We dye test tanks, check flappers and fill valves, and inspect supply lines and water heater fittings, with most of those fixed in under an hour from truck stock. They are inexpensive repairs.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Apex, NC

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Apex

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Crawlspace foundations are very common here, which makes many plumbing repairs easier to reach than they would be under a slab. We isolate the line first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak so nobody has to break the floor open to search, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before the saw comes out.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Apex

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell come up on most of our calls. All of them mean water. Thermal cameras find the wet area, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we cut a single neat access opening instead of chasing the stain across the wall.
