    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Apex

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Apex

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Apex, NC

    Building a home in Apex, NC? Adding a loop during framing is easy and cheap, since the plumber can route the main through the right spot before drywall goes up. We work with builders. Hose bibs can stay on hard water.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Apex

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Water Softener Replacement in Apex, NC

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Apex, NC

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Filters add cost. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Apex

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.
